# Implemented mathematical approach

The public data preserve all seven original ASCII exports as float64 arrays with batch1, point and two-column axes. No normalization, denoising, artificial mixture or target-dependent calibration is performed. Native ReadData chooses the floor indices at11870 and12030eV inclusive, giving160sample points. Piecewise-linear interpolation evaluates each independent standard on these sample energies; selected energies are inside every original interval. Coordinates remain absolute eV. The signal is dimensionless edge-normalized absorption. Exact export time/concentration are unavailable.

For six weights w and the six-by160 standard matrix S, predicted absorption is f_i=sum_j w_j S_ji. Source arithmetic uses six sequential products and additions. We preserve that operation order: changing to a BLAS product perturbed one MINPACK boundary-start trajectory enough to violate a pre-frozen parity tolerance. This is floating-point trajectory sensitivity, not a different physical model.

MODELS enumerates (s1,s2), each of four triples containing these two, and each of six quadruples. The historical source orders quadruples 1234,1235,1236,1245,1256,1246. All are executed. For a subset with k+1 standards, the first k weights are free; their internal variables u are mapped as w=(sin(u)+1)/2. Other weights remain zero. The last weight is the sequential expression 1-w1-w2-..., clipped to[0,1], matching native lmfit expression bounds. This clipping does NOT enforce a simplex when the free sum exceeds1; it is retained for exact upstream parity. Final selected weights are explicitly checked for simplex membership. Intermediate model9 can approach a clipped corner; do not interpret its algorithmic limit as a chemical detection.

The objective is the sum of squared residuals r_i=(mu_i-f_i)/0.001, with no regularizer. The scale0.001 is the example's assumed common normalization, not independently measured uncertainty. It leaves optimum weights and model ranking unchanged relative to an unscaled uniform least-squares loss. All original residual degrees of freedom are n-k, n=160. Statistics: chi=sum r²; reduced chi=chi/(n-k); AIC=n log(chi/n)+2k; BIC=n log(chi/n)+k log n. AIC/BIC are comparative scores under common assumed error scaling, not proof of a complete noise model. The minimum reduced chi chooses index7 (one-based8). Source and task use no energy-shift parameter.

Initialization: all fits start physical s1=s2=.4; newly free third weight0. The binary has only first weight. Model(1,2,5,6) instead initializes its three free weights .3 each. Convert to internal u=arcsin(2w-1). scipy.optimize.leastsq runs MINPACK Levenberg-Marquardt finite-difference updates: solve damped least-squares linearization in u, adjusting trust-region damping by reduction. ftol=xtol=1.5e-8, gtol=0, epsfcn=1e-10, factor100, maxfev=4000(k+1). No custom gradient or alternative optimizer is substituted. Successful MINPACK codes1–4 accepted; others raise. Native lmfit may count extra residual evaluations separately. Functions preserve epsilon, bounds, initialization, stopping and numerical evaluation order.

Independent checks use the affine physical derivative dr/dw_j=-(S_j-S_last)/0.001, finite differences and direct unconstrained linear least squares for the selected interior solution. These are tests/diagnostics, not replacement production solvers. The notebook also recomputes all eleven models, full six-standard unconstrained affine fit and shifts standard axes by +/-0.2eV as explicitly labeled calibration sensitivity experiments. No perturbation is a benchmark observation.

No independent chemical truth exists. The hidden reference is the native result, clearly identified as a method reference. Frozen absolute numerical tolerances are weightmax1e-4, spectrumRMSE1e-5, forwardconsistencyRMSE1e-6, measuredfitRMSE.0037, simplexerror1e-6. No NCC is applied to the short sparse coefficient vector. RMS uses sqrt(mean squared difference), without dynamic-range normalization. All outputs must be real floating and finite, weights[0,1]. Overflow is an evaluator error. Numerical agreement is not proof of unique chemical composition; hydroxide can compensate pH/hydration mismatch and sulfur standards overlap.
