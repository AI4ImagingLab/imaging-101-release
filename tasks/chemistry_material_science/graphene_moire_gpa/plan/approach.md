# Implemented mathematics

## Evaluation contract revision 2 (2026-10-29)

The solver and archived upstream arrays below retain their original ten-step PCG implementation. The released task now evaluates the explicitly specified weighted-gradient normal equations rather than demanding agreement with that finite iteration protocol. Its derived lock-in observations, carrier neighborhood, Fourier Gaussian bandwidth, amplitude weighting, wrapped projected gradients, edge confidence and zero-mean gauge are public in README. The independent evaluator rebuilds these quantities from raw observations and tests the maximum component relative normal-equation residual (boundary 0.001) and mean displacement (boundary 1e-8 nm). No hidden displacement array participates in version 2 scoring. Historical NCC/NRMSE and upstream parity measurements remain reproduction evidence for the reference implementation, not version 2 acceptance criteria. A more converged integration is a valid submission.

The unknown u=(u_row,u_column) is the inverse displacement of moire reflection contrast relative to image-defined reference waves. The acquisition is a real512-square calibrated LEEM crop,0.88nm/pixel. Image coordinates are integer row,column with increasing row downward. The model is I(r)=B(r)+sum_j A_j(r)cos[2pi k_j dot(r-u(r))+phi_j]+n(r). This is an effective moire contrast representation, not an independent atomic-layer elasticity inversion. The three k-vectors are visible-image Fourier peaks selected by original extract_primary_ks with period range10–100pixels. They are legitimate public observation-derived reference carriers, not target-derived calibration. The zero-displacement origin and an independently unstrained atomic lattice are not measured.

Preprocessing converts uint16 counts exactly to float64 and subtracts the global image mean. For a trial carrier w, spatial lock-in L_w(r)=IFFT(G_sigma FFT[(I-mean I)exp(2pi i w dot r)]). G_sigma is the real Gaussian transfer function with standard deviation sigma in real-space pixels, evaluated by scipy.ndimage.fourier_gaussian. This implies periodic extension of the finite image; no padding, real-space boundary replacement, deconvolution or interpolation is introduced. NumPy FFT signs/normalization match original GPA.

Let k denote one of the three declared carriers, nu_j=norm(k_j), kw=mean(nu)/2.5, kstep=kw/3 and sigma=ceil(1/min(nu))=33pixels. Evaluate w_row in arange(k_row-kw,k_row+kw,kstep) and similarly w_column, preserving ascending row-outer,column-inner order. Each complex candidate is compensated by exp[-2pi i(w-k) dot r]. At each pixel select the candidate with strictly largest amplitude; ties retain the earlier candidate. This is the original adaptive wfr2 algorithm. The selected phase is angle(L) and selected carrier maps are validated independently.

For carrier j form wrapped phase differences b_x=wrap(diff_column angle L_j) and b_y=wrap(diff_row angle L_j), where wrap(v)=(v+pi) modulo2pi-pi. They satisfy K Delta u approximately b with K_j=2pi k_j. At each pixel solve min_v sum_j [w_j(K_j dot v-b_j)]^2. The weight is abs(L_j) times an edge factor:1.000001 in the interior row/column66:-66 and1e-6 elsewhere. As in upstream, use the leading pixel's weight at each derivative edge, not an average of adjacent weights. Local code evaluates the explicit2x2 normal equations; independent parity checks ensure equivalence to upstream per-pixel SVD least squares. Positive determinant is required.

Cartesian derivative fields are integrated separately. Let D_x,D_y be nearest-neighbour forward differences. With W=norm_j(w_j)^2, edge weights are W_x=min(W_left,W_right), W_y=min(W_top,W_bottom). Minimize one half sum_edges W_axis(D_axis u-g_axis)^2. Natural boundary conditions follow from zero-padded divergence. The implemented negative-semidefinite normal operator Q(p)=div(W_x D_x p,W_y D_y p), and right side r=div(W_x wrap(g_x),W_y wrap(g_y)), preserve the upstream gradient re-wrapping convention. Both sides use the same sign, yielding the correct integration, independently checked by an affine displacement field.

Start u=0 and r=right side. Precondition with the Neumann Poisson inverse: DCTn(r)/[2(cos(pi i/N)+cos(pi j/N)-2)], then inverse DCTn, using scale(0,0)=1 to preserve bias. This bound dataset is square512x512. Upstream incorrectly exchanges row/column denominators for general rectangles; the selected square case is mathematically unaffected. Local code explicitly rejects nonsquare integration rather than silently generalizing the flawed upstream expression. With z=P_inverse r, rho=sum(r*z), use p=z initially or z+(rho/rho_previous)p thereafter; alpha=rho/sum(p*Qp); u+=alpha*p; r-=alpha*Qp. Stop at10iterations or residual norm<1e-9 times initial norm, or exactly zero residual. This retains the source's fixed approximate integration rather than substituting a fully converged solver. It fixes displacement gauge to numerical zero mean.

Convert reconstructed pixel displacement to nanometres by multiplying0.88. No post-hoc phase fitting, scale adjustment, alignment, crop or gauge removal is performed by evaluation. All two components and all512-square pixels are compared. There is no actual displacement truth: hidden reference arrays are the independently executed upstream result, not physical truth. NCC is flattened cosine similarity and NRMSE is RMSE divided by reference dynamic range. Nonzero NRMSE<=1e-6 and NCC>=0.999999 boundaries are justified by independently measured float64 port agreement (maximum2.13e-14nm) and reject zero/poor fields.

Notebook experiments recompute fixed-carrier lock-in, sigma23 and sigma46 windows on the same measured image. These expose adaptation/window dependence; they do not claim error against unavailable physical truth. A derived row-strain RMS is the interior derivative du_row/drow_nm, evaluated80pixels from edges and labelled relative moire strain. Additional source-paper multi-domain, thermal and atomic heterostrain experiments are outside this selected bound observation and are not represented by placeholders.

On this crop all active rewrap locations are in the66pixel weak edge (869 gx and679 gy), and both interior maxima are below pi (0.620618059858323 and0.4983523090338092pixel).
