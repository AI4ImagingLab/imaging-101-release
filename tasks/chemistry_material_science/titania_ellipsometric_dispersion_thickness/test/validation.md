## Mathematics, conventions and source-to-local traceability

The complex passive convention is n+i*k and rho=rp/rs=tan(Psi)exp(-iDelta). Delta differences are circular. Layer order is air/TiO2/fixedSiO2/Si, with semi-infinite air and substrate.
