# Implemented mathematics

The bound problem is experimental artificial-spin-ice electron phase retrieval, followed by projected induction. The 2021 paper introduces PyLorentz and its TIE magnetic/electrostatic separation (Eq.13 and adjacent discussion); the official 2024 demo supplies this different real experimental specimen. No independent truth or thickness is available. The four outputs are standard-method estimates, never magnetization or local B.

Let Uj,Fj be the unflipped/flipped float32 intensity images, j=0,...,6, with lens defocus [-928972,-398131,-199065,0,199065,398131,928972] nm. The native 512-square crop is not resampled. For pair index n=0,1,2, choose j-=2-n and j+=4+n, defocus d=199065,398131,928972 nm respectively. Main uses n=0, no Tikhonov filtering and periodic boundaries. The common in-focus image is (U3+F3)/2. Form the five-image stack [Uj-,Fj-,I0,Uj+,Fj+]. Multiply each plane by max(total intensity)/its total intensity, divide the entire stack by its largest pixel, then add 1e-9. Preserve float32 sums and operations. These are normalized detector units; the small floor is a source numerical convention.

Define raw central differences DB=((U+-U-)-(F+-F-))/2 and DE=((U+-U-)+(F+-F-))/2 using the normalized stack, then subtract each difference's spatial mean. They are intensity differences, not yet divided by 2d. Thus the physical lens-defocus derivatives are DB/(2d), DE/(2d). Specimen inversion reverses magnetic phase only. No target-derived mask is used; the mask is identically one.

With voltage V=200000 V, λ=h·1e9/sqrt(2me e)/sqrt(V+eV²/(2me c²)) nm. Here h is Planck constant, me electron mass, e positive elementary charge and c light speed. Source microscope transfer is exp(+iπλdf|k|²), whereas the paper's propagation-coordinate TIE uses the opposite sign. In the lens convention, derivative I'=+λ/(2π)div(I0 gradφ). This convention is independently tested against coherent wave propagation, not inferred solely from matching two inverse implementations.

For q=(qx,qy) in cycles/pixel, define Q=1/|q|², Q(0)=0. Optional source Tikhonov uses Q=|q|²/(|q|²+(qc·s)²)² with pixel pitch s=5.1066 nm and qc in cycles/nm, zero mode again zero. A(q) is the fixed circular aperture of radius Ny/3 on the centered integer frequency grid, shifted to FFT order. This is the source's two-thirds Nyquist-diameter convention. Let H(f)=IFFT(AQ FFT(f)). Centered periodic pixel gradients are Gy f=(f[y+1]-f[y-1])/2 and Gx analogously; their adjoints are their negatives.

For D equal DB or DE, evaluate u=-H(D), vj=real(Gj u/I0), t=Gy vy+Gx vx, and

```math
\phi=-\frac{s^2}{16\pi^3\lambda d}\operatorname{Re}H(t).
```

This directly implements the source's double inverse Laplacian including normalization, sign, aperture and centered gradient attenuation. It is Teague's auxiliary-potential approximation for spatially varying I0; no generic optimization is substituted. There is no iterative optimizer, initializer, step size or stopping tolerance: the FFT inverse is closed form. Each phase is shifted by its own minimum after cropping, fixing its unobservable constant. Optional even boundary extension mirrors the selected stack into a 1024-square float64 array before computing differences and their means, reconstructs, crops the original top-left 512-square ROI, then fixes phase gauge. This reproduces the original order and promotion, rather than mirroring an already rounded derivative.

Projected induction is Bx=ℏ/(e s)·1e18 Gy φB and By=-ℏ/(e s)·1e18 Gx φB in T·nm. These derivatives use NumPy gradient with edge_order=2: centered interior differences and second-order one-sided edges, distinct from the periodic inversion stencils. Source calculates gradients before gauge fixing; the local constant shift changes derivatives by <6e-13 T·nm. Both conventions are tested. The mapping includes all magnetic flux along the beam and stray-field path, without division by guessed thickness.

The notebook actively computes the standard solution, all three focus pairs, qc=0.0002,0.0005,0.001 cycles/nm, mirrored boundaries, and a flip-sign control. No fresh curve is an archived substitute. Source example comparisons retained here are TIE magnetic/electrostatic separation, phase/induction visualization, focal-pair selection, optional Tikhonov and boundary sensitivity. The distinct CGT single-image SITIE demo is not this acquisition or task; it is explicitly excluded rather than mislabeled as a comparison on these observations.

Evaluation uses both phases and both induction components with standard uncentered cosine NCC and dynamic-range NRMSE. There is no alignment.
