# Implemented mathematical approach

Temperature obeys ∂tT=α∂zzT−hT on each outward branch. For the convention Re[U(z)exp(iωt)], U=Cexp(−kz), k²=(h+iω)/α, ω=2πf. Write k=a+ib with a,b positive. Imaginary parts give 2ab=ω/α and therefore α=πf/(ab). Log amplitude and phase slopes are −a and −b. A negative/positive unphysical slope product is not expected here; the original absolute-value convention is preserved. No actual physical truth is available; method agreement is not an accuracy claim.

Native float64 arrays are clipped to at least .001mK, phase is multiplied by π/180, and both images are rotated90° counterclockwise (index operation). Pixel scale remains .0013mm. The original GUI selections are fixed on observed images: vertical approximate line x=.1066mm, y=.2..1.4mm and fit bounds (.2,.6)mm. A 41-column region about x=82 is used to estimate the ridge: retain columns whose amplitude is at least0.7 times that row's maximum and greater than1.5 times the row mean, average their indices, regress these means on row index. Rotation angle is −atan(ridge slope), in degrees. This angle from acquisition1 is shared by acquisition2, preserving the original frequency-sweep convention.

Images are rotated with SciPy order1 interpolation, reshape=True and NaN exterior. The mean position of the100 largest finite amplitudes, rounded to nearest integer using NumPy, locates the source. Keep41 columns centered there. Source profiles use the central column, not a transverse average; the original program's zero-width averaging parameter is preserved. Increasing-row and decreasing-row branches begin at the observed source and use spacing .0013mm. Profile arrays extend to730/731 positions with NaN padding where a branch ends; fitting excludes nonfinite points. This padding is diagnostic, never a submitted unknown.

For distance samples x and an observed profile y, unweighted ordinary least squares evaluates m=Σ(x−mean(x))(y−mean(y))/Σ(x−mean(x))² and c=mean(y)−m mean(x). It minimizes Σ(y−mx−c)² without regularization. Searchsorted selects [0.2,0.6)mm, giving308 pixels. Slope standard error is sqrt[Σ residual²/(n−2)/Σ(x−mean(x))²]. Local diffusivities use πf/|mA mP|. For the joint result regress all four products p=mA mP against their frequencies through the origin: q=Σfp/Σf², αjoint=|π/q|. The original generic curve-fit implementation is replaced by the equivalent explicit one-parameter formula, agreeing to1.13e−11 relative. No optimizer initialization, iterations, learning rate or stopping criteria apply to these closed forms.

Lossless amplitude-only πf/mA² and phase-only πf/mP² estimates expose heat-loss bias. These are supplementary comparisons; the required submission remains the selected product estimator. No extra material or power sweep is invented.
