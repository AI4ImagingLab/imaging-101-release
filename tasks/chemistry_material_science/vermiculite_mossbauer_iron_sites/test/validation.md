## Local mathematics and parity
Core transmission and inverse mathematics are local explicit executable equations. For the selected isotropic zero-Gaussian powder branch, the complex nuclear cross-section is a scalar matrix. Source exp(-S/2) amplitude followed by unpolarized Gram readout equals exp(-realS); its dispersive phase cancels exactly. Extracting this analytic scalar reduction preserves full-thickness transmission, not an easier thin-spectrum substitute. The Lorentzian source,64node map/Jacobian and finite-wing photons are retained. No domain solver, source import, cross-task import or opaque matrix library is used; NumPy array algebra is general computation. LM damping, finite differences, step clipping, auto-fixing, bound recheck and stopping are explicitly implemented rather than replaced by scipy.optimize.
