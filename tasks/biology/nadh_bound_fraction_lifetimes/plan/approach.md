# Validated graphical two-component inversion

The local method is the official PhasorPy0.12 implementation of the 2020 two-component graphical method, not the historical closed SimFCS executable. It reconstructs full measured 512x512 cells using all97delaybins and the entire independent fluorescein reference. No training, seed, synthetic observations, learned calibration or target endpoint is used.

## Forward model and preprocessing

At harmonic h=1,2 and pixel p, z(h,p)=(1-f)/(1-i h omega tau-f)+f/(1-i h omega tau-b), where omega=2 pi*.08/ns. Lifetimes are ns, f is a dimensionless integrated intensity fraction. Monoexponential components lie on (g-.5)^2+s^2=.25, s>=0. DFT uses positive sine: conjugate of NumPy rfft, normalized by total intensity. Zero-total pixels have NaN internal phasors. Their released output values are zero. Pixel support is fixed solely by observed histogram sum>0 (92029 pixels).

The reference is intensity-pooled over all pixels before its first two normalized Fourier coefficients are computed. Multiply observed moment h by [1/(1-i*h*omega*3.05)]/reference(h). This is complex rotation and modulation calibration, not target-derived normalization. The histograms are prefiltered uint8 intensity values; no additional mask/filter/Poisson model is imposed. All computation is float64, complete97-bin periodic convention, no temporal or spatial padding.

## Search and projection

Version2 uses the closed mathematical grid 0.20+0.01k ns, k=0,...,60, evaluated as start plus step times an integer. The historical NumPy arange(.2,.8,.01) happened to produce61 candidates including0.8000000000000005ns. The endpoint is retained explicitly, while implementation-dependent rounding no longer defines acceptable lifetime ties. Each tau defines a=1/(1+(omega*tau)^2), b=sqrt(a-a^2). Construct the line through (a,b) and measured first-harmonic (r,s). Intersect it with the semicircle, sorting intersections by decreasing real coordinate so fast component comes first. The standard line-circle discriminant and determinant are evaluated directly in graphical_search. A negative discriminant or coincident direction follows original degenerate handling, fixes equal endpoints/fraction and ends that pixel's scan. Out-of-domain phasors or invalid upper-half intersections are skipped. All real observations here produce finite solutions.

Map each endpoint g to G=g/(4-3g) and S=sqrt(G-G^2). If v is the vector from fast to slow mapped endpoint, q the measured second-harmonic point minus the fast endpoint, projection f=(q dot v)/(v dot v). Candidate objective is squared perpendicular distance |q-f*v|^2, dimensionless. Strictly smaller objective replaces the stored candidate: ties retain the earlier candidate. There is no gradient descent, regularizer, line search or iterative stopping parameter; the finite candidate scan is exhaustive. Convert endpoints back by tau=sqrt((1-g)/(g*omega^2)). Return both lifetimes and complementary fractions (1-f,f). Do not silently substitute a least-squares four-moment fit. Under noise fractions projected separately at the two harmonics differ; the original method reports the second-harmonic projection. Its first-harmonic residual is independently retained in forward diagnostics.

## Required original comparison

single_harmonic fixes fast lifetime .4ns, obtains the intensity-weighted first-harmonic cell centroid, finds the other semicircle intersection of its line from the fast point, and projects each observed phasor onto that endpoint line, clipping fractions to [0,1]. The inferred slow endpoint is an estimated comparison parameter, never public calibration. The original tutorial flags a discrepancy with 2025 Fig6c. That paper discusses unnormalized distance from a .4ns endpoint separately from normalized intensity fraction and exponential amplitude fraction; we retain all distinction and make no claim of exact reproduction of Fig6c or its unpublished processing.

## Evaluation and sensitivity

No biological truth is available. The reference arrays are independently run original method outputs on exactly these observations, with background convention explicitly changed from NaN to zero outside observed support. Original rerun is bit-exact. Historical v1 froze output NRMSE<=1e-6 and maximum absolute error<=1e-5 independently for both quantities. These reference-identity criteria are superseded in v2 by per-pixel geometric tests: grid membership within1e-7ns, first-harmonic line residual<=1e-7, second-harmonic fraction projection discrepancy<=1e-7, and squared perpendicular objective no greater than the closed-grid attained minimum plus1e-12. The forward RMS boundary remains0.037770825912687135. Coincident second-harmonic endpoints with squared separation<=1e-20 leave fraction unidentifiable. No rescaling or cropping is allowed. The stored baseline is a fresh cleaned v2 method estimate, never physical truth; original upstream measurements remain distinct.

The notebook actively repeats both official approaches; plots all3unknowns, raw histograms, calibration, two-harmonic phasors, reference comparisons, forward residual distribution, grid boundary populations and long-lifetime tails; compares fresh .02ns grid and wider .1..1ns prior and omission of independent calibration. These are declared sensitivity experiments, not alternative benchmark outputs. Long slow-lifetime tails and many fast estimates on prior endpoints demonstrate limitations. Synthetic exact mixtures are unit/physics checks only, not benchmark data or truth.


## Explicit line-circle intersection conventions

Let candidate be (a,b), measured first-harmonic point (r,s), dx=r-a, dy=s-b, L=dx squared+dy squared and D=(a-.5)*s-(r-.5)*b. Set Q=.25*L-D squared. Intersection coordinates, relative to circle center (.5,0), are [(D*dy minus/plus sign(dy)*dx*sqrt(Q))/L, (-D*dx minus/plus abs(dy)*sqrt(Q))/L]. Add .5 to both real coordinates. sign follows NumPy copysign(1,dy), including positive zero. Sort the resulting two points by descending real coordinate. All these coordinates and lengths are dimensionless. Negative Q and nonpositive L use the original early-degenerate branch; candidate bounds and upper-half-plane conditions are checked before the second-harmonic objective. Strict smaller-distance selection and candidate ordering are essential to parity near ties.
