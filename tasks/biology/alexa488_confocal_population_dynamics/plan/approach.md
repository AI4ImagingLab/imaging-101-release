Gaussian lateral and axial detection profiles generate the rational/square-root diffusion kernel; the fixed aspect ratio is 5. The offset is zero and no detector background correction is applied. Absolute beam radius is unknown, preventing conversion to concentration or absolute diffusion. Native lag coordinates and float64 precision are preserved.

The objective is sum_i (G_i-g_i)^2, no one-half or sample normalization, no weighting, regularizer or fitted alignment. Bounds are N in [1e-6,1e5], T in [0,.95], d in [.002,1000] ms; the numerical positive N floor lies far outside the solution and approximates the public N>0 domain.
