# Implemented forward and inverse mathematics

The complete measured32×23×26 uint16 stack is decoded once into public NPZ. No synthetic observations, smoothing, renormalization or new crop are used. All598pixels are fitted independently. Angles are buffer incidence in radians, not angles in oxide/silicon. Heights and all optical lengths are nm; camera signal isADU. The visible README defines the complete TE transfer-matrix acquisition and its complex convention. `optical_constants` returns columns c=Re(r), d=Im(r), k=4πnb cos(theta)/lambda in nm^-1. Complex Snell sine uses NumPy's principal complex arcsine, verified against original Julia on all angles and independently at zero oxide thickness.

At a pixel let x=(A,B,H). Define P(H)=1+2c cos(kH)-2d sin(kH)+c²+d² and f(x)=A P(H)+B. Signal residual is e=f(x)-y. The minimized objective is the unnormalized sum S=eᵀe, inADU²; no1/N,1/2, noise weighting or spatial regularizer enters the actual algorithm. Jacobian columns are P(H),1, and -2Ak[c sin(kH)+d cos(kH)]. A and B have unitsADU and H has unitsnm. The third column has unitsADU/nm.

Initialization is exactly source `[0.5,1,75]`, with lower bounds `[0,0,0]` and upper bounds `[10000,10000,200]`. These define the native selected membrane example; no hidden field determines initialization. The source's analytic-Jacobian box-constrained LM is implemented explicitly, retaining the numerical rules of LsqFit0.15.1. At each iteration compute D=diag(max(sum over angles of J²,1e-6)) and solve `(JᵀJ+mu D)v=-Jᵀe`. Initialize mu=10. Clip x+v to bounds and redefine v to the clipped displacement. The predicted new residual is e+Jv. Define quality rho=(S_trial-S)/(S_predicted-S); an undefined ratio is not an accepted step. Accept only S_trial<S and rho>1e-3. Accepted rho>0.75 multiplies mu by0.1 down to1e-16; rejection multiplies mu by10 up to1e16. No generic optimizer replaces these source-specific updates.

Stop if the current iteration's Jacobian transposed times the retained residual has infinity norm below1e-12, or if norm(v)<1e-8(1e-8+norm(x)); otherwise stop at1000iterations. The Jacobian in this convergence check is deliberately the same one used in that trial, including after an accepted step, matching upstream. `fit_pixel` returns the retained x, attempted iteration count, convergence boolean and S. The original and fresh full fits both converge at every pixel; diagnostics are kept separate from required submission keys.

Output fields are full real float64 `(1,23,26)`: reconstruction=H, amplitude=A, background=B. There is no post-fit clipping, alignment, mean correction, smoothing, physical magnification or fitted scale. Source TIFF native pixels are preserved; lateral physical sampling is not supplied and is never invented. All three quantities are evaluated. The standard entry point reads only raw NPZ, not reference/fixture arrays.

The hidden baseline is an independent original numerical run, not physical truth. Cosine NCC has no mean subtraction; NRMSE is RMS error divided by baseline dynamic range. Before local implementation, per-parameter tolerance was frozen at rtol2e-6,atol2e-5 and prediction tolerance0.001ADU. Full-field Judge thresholds are NCC>=0.99999999 and NRMSE<=1e-5 for each field, with a nonzero numerical tolerance because the method supplies its own reference. This is stricter than meaningful scientific parameter differences but above independently measured numerical rerun/backend drift; it must not be interpreted as membrane metrology accuracy.

The independent Judge also computes full-stack RMS consistency using public intensities/angles and the declared calibrated optics; its boundary is1.05times frozen source RMS184.0961116623046ADU. The nonzero residual reflects camera noise/model mismatch, not a known noise standard deviation or independently established chi-square. The notebook shows its spatial/angular structure, parameter standard errors conditional on least-squares assumptions, initial-height ambiguity, calibration changes and controlled added signal noise. Perturbation differences are measured against the unperturbed fresh estimate, never labelled errors against GT. Altered calibration/start/noise experiments do not change the standard task's observation, constants or thresholds.
