## Exact warm-start and stopping details

The published EM_with_priors reinitializes both transition columns with fresh uniform(.2,.8) draws after warmup; it does not simply reuse the fixed transition. Noise is perturbed as .5*prior_noise+1.5*prior_noise*uniform(.1,1). OFF loading is multiplied by(.7+.6*uniform(.1,1))*uniform(.1,.2), ON by(.7+.6*uniform(.1,1))*uniform(.1,.6). The single seeded NumPy random stream is continued, not reset between stages. Prior initial-state probabilities are reused. The source retains old lambda_log and v_logs during this random warm-start until their first update; this original bookkeeping is preserved. Its Gaussian log-normalizer is common to all states at a frame, so this temporary normalizer choice cancels from posterior probabilities. Raw initial log-likelihood values are therefore not claimed as calibrated evidence.

Expected transitions are accumulated in log space and each current-state column is normalized by the sum over its two next states. Initial probabilities sum the first-frame posterior across traces and divide by63. For state-weight vector a(s)=(OFFweight,ONweight), the emission system sums posterior gamma(s,t) times a(s)a(s)-transpose into M and gamma(s,t)*y(t)*a(s) into b, then solves Mv=b. Noise variance sums gamma(s,t)*(y(t)-a(s)-transpose*v)^2 over all states and valid frames, divided by total valid framecount. Local signed-log calculations preserve the original operation order.

At each iteration compare relative changes in initial-vector norm, transition-matrix norm, noise standard deviation, emission-vector norm and absolute log-likelihood change divided by total observed framecount. The original fixed iteration/stopping branch is retained rather than inventing an objective monotonicity guarantee. Finite beam truncation and initial bookkeeping mean neither exact EM monotonicity nor independently true promoter labels are claimed.

Deterministic state/known-weight operator memoization is bounded, keyed by state,W,K,response values and requested trace length. It accelerates repeated forward-model basis evaluation while retaining every arithmetic formula; it neither loads references nor reuses an archived reconstruction.

The original general response helper indexes its first array axis while generating ramp weights and fails for kappa>=2. This task binds kappa1.5, where the original result matches the independent finite-exposure formula. The local helper explicitly rejects kappa>=2 rather than advertising a validated generic ramp implementation. A separate1.75case checks memoization keys within the supported original branch. No bound observation or selected discretization changed.

The training objective maximizes the sum, over all traces, of the log of the retained-path Gaussian-HMM marginal likelihood. Each path contributes its initial probability, successive column-stochastic transition probabilities and Gaussian observation densities. There is no per-frame objective normalization, additional regularizer or Bayesian parameter-prior penalty: the method names with_priors refer to warm-start parameter estimates, not a posterior distribution over parameters. Only the stopping statistic divides log-likelihood change by totalframecount. Relative norm changes use Euclidean2norm for initial/emission vectors and spectral matrix2norm for transitions, with absolute relative scalar change for noise SD.
