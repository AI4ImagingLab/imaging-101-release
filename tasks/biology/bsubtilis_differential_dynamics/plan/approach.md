## Image structure function

For each frame I, compute F=rfft2(I)/sqrt(HW), H=W=128, and shift only the y frequency axis. For lag l=1,...,T-1, D(l,k)=sum(t=0..T-l-1)|F(t+l,k)-F(t,k)|²/(T-l). The implemented FFT equivalent uses temporal FFT length2T. If z is the complex series at one spatial mode, ac(l)=real(ifft(|fft(z,2T)|²))[l], then D=[sum of first T-l |z|² + sum of last T-l |z|² -2ac(l)]/(T-l). Two cumulative sums preserve upstream addition order. Last two packed planes are mean |z|² and variance z. No periodic temporal pairs are used. Process independent Fourier columns in chunks256 to bound memory, preserving all modes and exact per-mode operations. Stage parity is exact after retaining original floating operation order tau=l*(1/fs) and k*=1/d; algebraically equivalent division produced tiny off-signal fit differences and was corrected before freezing, not accommodated by threshold changes.

For radii rho=sqrt(kx²+ky²), use90 edges linspace(0,90*kx[1],90), left searchsorted insertion bin. Remove first x column and central y row and bin90. Real-FFT multiplicity w is2 exceptDC/Nyquist columns1. Mean is sum(wD)/sum(w). Spatial SD is sqrt(sum[w(D-mean)²]/[sum(w)-sum(w)/sum(w)]), retaining the original full-plane counting convention sumw2=sum(w), not sum(w²). This is spatial sample dispersion, not an independently calibrated temporal SEM. Empty bins are NaN, excluded from the returned finite physical range. Ring centers are weighted mean rho. Output selection is q in[.75,4] rad/micrometer, fixed before original execution, exactly39 bins.

Use the original quasi-log integer lag algorithm to choose200 monotonically increasing lags up to approximately half the record. Quadratically interpolate log(D) and log(SD) against log(tau), then exponentiate. These selected delays guide fits; the complete1999-delay DDM is nevertheless actively computed and used for preprocessing/noise estimation. Interpolation, finite overlaps and SD definitions reproduce the source.

## Objective, initialization, constraints and updates

The explicit acquisition relaxation model is g(tau;p)=A[1-exp(-(Gamma*tau)^beta)]+B. A,Gamma>=machineepsilon, beta>=1; B is unbounded. Near-coverslip-labelled recording fits beta; second recording fixes beta1.35. It is an empirical ensemble relaxation model; no unique microscopic velocity distribution or object-level truth is inferred. Initial B is twice the mean of the last7 radial temporal variance bins. Initial A(q)=2variance(q)-B. At reference bin10, find tau closest to normalized correlation exp(-1), set Gamma=1/tau, beta1.35. The first reference-bin fit is unweighted, exactly as original fit_multik. Then warm-start fits in descending bins9..0 and ascending bins10..89, including the second reference fit. Other fits minimize sum[((D-g)/SD)²]. No additional regularizer is used.

Local lower-bound transformation for physical variable a>=L is a=L-1+sqrt(u²+1), inverse u=sqrt((a-L+1)²-1). B stays untransformed. This explicitly reproduces the original lmfit bounds; core model, residuals, ordering and transforms are local, with scipy.optimize.leastsq only supplying general MINPACK LM.
