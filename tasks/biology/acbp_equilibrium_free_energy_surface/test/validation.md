## Explicit mathematical and physical audit

The complete model is G=H(1−T/M)+C(T−M−Tlog(T/M))−mD, with temperatures inKelvin, energykJ/mol,R=.008314kJ/(mol K), m1=m2=0. Two independent7-coefficient optical baseline models share one equilibrium population. Native normalization is intensity divided by each own-channel/sample nearest70°C observation; it is public observation-derived preprocessing, not calibration from a hidden target. Its scale-gauge invariance is independently tested.

Independent tests prove G(M,0)=0 and u=.5 there; dG/dD=−m; increasing denaturant increases unfolded fraction; Gibbs–Helmholtz G−T(dG/dT)=H atM; d²G/dT²=−C/T; explicit optical mixtures and baseline coefficients agree with hand-known cases. All supplied source constants, axes and initial parameters are checked against frozen actual source outputs. Native negative-energy overflow guard clips only G<−250 inside exponentiation; fitted complete-domain minimum−109.014 means it is inactive, while reported energy remains unclipped. Every one of the95430 native observations participates in the objective and forward consistency metric.

Known **postfit plotting discrepancy**, outside core inference: the original author notebook defines a purported normalized observed fraction as I/model/(1+exp(G)). The exponent is dimensional and omits RT; it also does not algebraically unmix optical baselines. The cleaned core does not copy that formula as physics. The active notebook explicitly retains this legacy formula and its window-selected.5point annotations as diagnostics, compares it to physically correct u=1/(1+exp(G/RT)) and algebraic observed baseline-unmixing(I−F)/(U−F), and plots the model G=0midpoint contour. No legacy wrong plot is relabeled physical truth. Window annotation tolerance is explicitly source empirical plotting convention, not a calibrated statistical acceptance criterion. Baseline-unmixed observations can be unstable at low contrast and are not used as calibration, targets, evaluation masks or solver input.
