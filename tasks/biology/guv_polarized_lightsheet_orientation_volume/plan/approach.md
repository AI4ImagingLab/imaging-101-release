# Implemented mathematics

This task implements the published real-GUV ALL-state Tikhonov baseline, qualified solely to complete odd-grid negative-frequency coverage. The original unmodified source and failed odd-grid result are retained privately. The task returns all15 real spherical-harmonic coefficient fields, not an independently validated molecular distribution. It retains signed values, null-space regularization, observation background and all spatial support.

## Data and calibration

Observations are exact lossless crops of the author's already registered/deskewed acquisition. The author script specifies X128:205,Y705:760,Z56:136 before reconstruction; no additional downsampling or support selection is made. The registered unit is130nm on all axes. Input counts have axes(batch,X,Y,Z,state,view), with21 states per view ordered as tilt0,+1,−1, each containing seven polarizer angles0,45,60,90,120,135,180degrees. ALL uses indices0,2,...,20 in each view, reflecting the author's explicitly stated state1 registration issue. Other groups are retained as notebook comparisons: SIX-NO-TILT indices[0,2,5] for each view, eta.1; SIX-WITH-TILT indicesA[7,9,11],B[2,11,18], eta.7.

Lake calibration is an independent same-session fluorescent-slide measurement. The original central30³ lake cube is preserved as uint16 counts. Convert it to float32 before reductions. For each of six tilt/view curves, average all XYZ samples and solve the seven-by-three least-squares problem with columns1,cos2θ,sin2θ. Evaluate the fitted curve on1000 evenly spaced angles0..180degrees, choose the first argmax and average the six maxima. The resulting bound-data offset is11.651651651651653degrees; this is actively recomputed, not inserted as target-derived calibration. Preserve the original averaging and argmax conventions.

Subtract that offset from each nominal polarizer angle. Construct the polarization vectors with the measured tilt rotations: A[0,+8.9,−9.2]degrees and B[0,+7.7,−6.5]degrees. Normalize them. Convert observations by dividing uint16 counts by65535 and casting float32, then select the acquisition group. Let C be the selected calibration means of shape(2,P), h be unit-isotropic model means of shape(P,2), globally normalized by their maximum. Compute corrected g as `g*C[0,0]*h/C.T` in that exact source arithmetic order. This promotes to float64 through h.

The published SI normalizes the model response by its first component, whereas the author script globally normalizes its uniform forward prediction by its maximum. Both apply the same relative calibration factors, with a single known global scale difference. The task deliberately preserves the author script's scale; it does not infer absolute concentration or fit gain from the target specimen. The filename LS_488 records the calibration channel;561nm is the author's selected effective transfer-function wavelength.

## Angular model

Use orthonormal real harmonics in lexicographic even-degree order l0,2,4 and increasing m. The complex Condon–Shortley functions are converted with the exact real convention in README; Y00=1/sqrt(4π). Illumination coefficients are Y00(p) and .4Y2m(p), which independently reconstruct3(p·s)²/(4π). No l4 illumination term is added.

Detection has no polarizer. For normalized transverse radius r, u=min(|r|/2,1), define A1=2/π(acos(u)−u sqrt(1−u²)) and A2=2/π(acos(u)−(3−r²/2)u sqrt(1−u²)). Outside radius2 the response vanishes. The detector's only nonzero SH entries before rotation are d0=A1+alpha²A2/4 and d20=(−A1+alpha²A2/2)/sqrt5, alpha=NA/1.33. The x-detection view rotates the six coefficients by the explicit90degree y-rotation matrix in `rotate_quadrupole`; after rotation detector indices0,3,5 may be nonzero.

Angular multiplication uses G[j,k,l]=integral YjYkYl dΩ for output j0..14, illumination k0..5, and detector l indices0,3,5. Instead of importing the source's precomputed Gaunt array or a domain package, the local code evaluates these exact finite-degree integrals using12-point Gauss–Legendre quadrature in cos(theta) and32 uniformly spaced azimuth points. All products have degree at most8 here; these rules resolve that finite polynomial/trigonometric space. Values below1e−14 are analytic selection-rule zeros represented with quadrature roundoff and are set to zero. Independent surface integration and source tensor fixtures verify the used kernel to2e−15 absolute tolerance. No claim is made about unused polarized-detector branches of the original library.

## Spatial acquisition operator and precision

Spatial Fourier frequencies have units nm−1. Multiply each by561/NA to obtain dimensionless normalized frequencies for its view. View A has NA1.1 and a transverse XY response; view B has NA.67 and a transverse YZ response. The remaining axial frequencies have Gaussian response exp(−q²/(2*.25²)). The source view-A axial values are float32 and view-B axial values are float64. Angular-transverse arrays are divided by the original microscope Hnorm, cast float32, then divided by their respective maximum absolute entries. Hnorm comes from the source's normalization illumination with coefficients[1,0,0,−1/sqrt5,0,0]/sqrt(4π), rotated for x illumination, multiplied by the zero-frequency detector.

These factorizations give H0(x,y,z,j,p)=Hz[z]Hxy[x,y,j,p] and H1(x,y,z,j,p)=Hx[x]Hyz[y,z,j,p]. Stack views on the final axis and flatten measurement axes in(P,view) order to obtain a15-by-2P matrix. Absolute FFT-frequency indices pair the positive factors with every signed X/Y frequency. For odd N the negative count is floor((N−1)/2), and all conjugate partners are retained. The original source excluded the highest negative bins on odd X and Y; changing only that coverage is a documented mathematical qualification.

The forward model FFTs the15 real coefficient fields, contracts H with the coefficient vector, stores detector Fourier coefficients as complex64, and inverse-transforms to float64 observations. Original NumPy1.26 promoted complex64 inverse FFT inputs; NumPy2 preserves input precision, so the local implementation explicitly casts to complex128 before inverse FFT. This is necessary to preserve the original double-precision image means. The linear forward operator has no target-dependent intensity normalization. Only the uniform specimen used for calibration has its prediction divided by the global maximum, as in the original script.

## Inverse and objective

At every spatial frequency, let H=U diag(s) Vh have coefficient rows and measurement columns. The forward equation is G=H^T F because the factors are real. Minimize ||H^T F−G||²+eta||F||². For eta>0 this conditional objective has a unique regularized solution; it does not resolve the unregularized angular null space as biological truth. Compute U diag(s/(s²+eta)) Vh G, suppressing source singular values s<=1e−7. The standard ALL eta is.7. No iterative optimizer, initial guess, convergence criterion, positivity projection or additional regularizer is introduced.

The local inverse computes SVDs on the nonnegative transverse quadrant and reflects its pseudoinverse by absolute frequency indices. As in source, multiply using the einsum contraction order, store reconstructed Fourier coefficients as complex64, then explicitly promote to complex128 for the real inverse FFT. Returned coefficient fields are float64. No output clipping or fitted alignment occurs.

## Evaluation and review

Each of15 fields is separately scored with cosine NCC without mean subtraction, RMS error divided by its reference dynamic range, and maximum absolute error. ALL fields vary on the bound observations, so those metrics are defined. Their fixed limits are NCC>=.999999, DR-NRMSE<=1e−6 and maxabs<=8e−9. The original and qualified independent repeats are bitwise identical. Before local extraction, an independently implemented full signed-frequency float64 normal-equation solution differed from qualified source by max2.873e−10; storing fields as float32 then restoring float64 changed them by max9.293e−10; four float32 ULP at the largest source coefficient are7.451e−9. These measurements justify the frozen nonzero8e−9 bound; per-field NRMSE protects weak coefficients.

The complete calibrated40-state forward relative L2 residual must not exceed.2919, against source.2889729727. There is no support mask, intensity fit, translation or angular reorientation in scoring.
