# Implemented forward and inverse model

All 360 measured spectra are fitted independently in the paper's no-flat-field condition. The observation index j runs from 0 to 359, corresponding to files spectrum_00316 through spectrum_00675. Detector wavelength lambda has 2048 float64 samples in nm. Subtract the measured dark spectrum, compute the mean dark-subtracted intensity over 280 <= lambda <= 290 nm, subtract this scalar stray-light level, and retain 310 <= lambda <= 320 nm. Preserve the original rowwise arithmetic order: changing the reduction order changes the ill-conditioned fit. There is no smoothing, rebinning, despiking, flat-field division or observation removal.

The independent solar spectrum F and three cross sections are sampled on mu = arange(309, 321 + .01, .01), retaining the original 1201 floating-point grid values. The upstream calibration loader uses cubic spline interpolation of high-resolution external spectra. SO2 at 295 K and O3 at 243 K have cross sections in cm^2/molecule. The Ring pseudo-cross-section is dimensionless. The 13 fitted parameters, in order, are SO2 column a, O3 column b, Ring amplitude r, four broadband polynomial coefficients c3,c2,c1,c0, wavelength stretch s, wavelength shift d, and four instrument-profile parameters FWEM,k,aw,ak.

Define B(mu)=c3*mu^3+c2*mu^2+c1*mu+c0, converting native solar units to detector counts. Define optical depth tau(mu)=a*sigmaSO2(mu)+b*sigmaO3(mu)+r*sigmaRing(mu). The high-resolution intensity is F(mu)*B(mu)*exp(-tau(mu)). Convolve this intensity once with the instrument profile; do not convolve the gases independently. Columns a and b have molecules/cm^2, r is dimensionless.

For profile samples x=arange(-2,2,.01) nm and w=FWEM/2, use A=k/(2*w*Gamma(1/k)). The unnormalized profile is A*exp(-abs(x/(w-aw))**(k-ak)) for x<=0 and A*exp(-abs(x/(w+aw))**(k+ak)) otherwise. Normalize by the discrete sample sum. FWEM, w and aw have nm; k and ak are dimensionless. The 400-sample even kernel uses numpy.convolve(...,'same'), including its native alignment and zero extension. Register mu'=mu+s*(mu-min(mu))+d and use cubic griddata interpolation onto the detector fit grid. The additive offset is fixed to zero; light dilution and flat calibration are absent in this selected published condition.

Minimize sum(counts-model)^2 over retained detector pixels. There is no weighting, mean normalization, half factor or regularizer. Each independent cold start is [1e16,1e19,.1,0,0,0,1,0,-.1,.6,2,0,0]. The O3 initialization and shift follow paper Table 1. scipy.optimize.curve_fit uses unconstrained MINPACK Levenberg-Marquardt, numerical finite differences, default ftol and xtol approximately 1.49e-8, gtol=0 and its default evaluation limit. There are no custom inverse updates, line searches or warm starts. Failed optimization is an error, never a reason to omit an observation.

Native covariance warnings occur because the polynomial and profile nuisance parameters are poorly conditioned. Covariance-derived errors can be infinite and are not validated uncertainty estimates. The fitted spectra and SO2 columns remain finite. The output is the SO2 profile only; the other parameters are nuisance quantities retained for diagnostics. Small negative clear-sky columns are allowed by the unconstrained noisy fit.

The notebook's additional DOAS diagnostic uses the mean first 20 measured spectra as its reference, mean fitted profile/registration, I0-corrected differential absorption columns at scales [1e18,1e19,.1], and a centered cubic polynomial. Ordinary linear least squares yields relative SO2 columns. This diagnostic is not a QDOAS reproduction and its estimated profile is not supplied as public calibration. Additional fresh fits vary initial profile width, and a no-SO2 forward ablation isolates the absorption signal.

Evaluation uses cosine NCC and RMS error divided by the reference dynamic range. The zero estimate has NCC=0. The fixed boundaries NCC>=.9999 and NRMSE<=1e-4 assess numerical reproduction of the original method, not accuracy against unavailable ground truth. No output scaling, clipping or alignment is allowed. Independent operator checks and residual plots provide separate physical evidence.
