# Implemented mathematics

Depth z and coil spacing s are meters; conductivity sigma and data a are mS/m. At each of 30 stations, seven piecewise constant layers have interfaces d=(0,.25,.5,.75,1,1.5,2,infinity). Cumulative HCP sensitivity is (1+4(z/s)^2)^(-1/2), VCP sensitivity is sqrt(1+4(z/s)^2)-2z/s. Their differences at consecutive interfaces form a 6 by 7 Jacobian J. C(infinity)=0, so the bottom column is C(2). Ground-contact height is zero, matching all supplied coil identifiers. No elevation, frequency or permeability factor is reapplied to the already calibrated apparent conductivities.

The implemented objective is sqrt(sum((J sigma-a)^2)/6 + alpha sum((sigma[j+1]-sigma[j])^2)/7). alpha=.07. Bounds are 0<=sigma<=300 mS/m. These are reference solver hyperparameters, not acquisition information. Each station starts with every conductivity20 mS/m. scipy.optimize.minimize uses L-BFGS-B, finite-difference derivatives, ftol=1e-12,gtol=1e-8,maxiter=1000. The objective, scalar normalization and square root reproduce the selected upstream method. The square root is monotone, so the bounded optimum also minimizes its internal quadratic. No lateral regularization, robust penalty, stochastic sampling or global training is used. The 2D section stacks independent depth profiles at actual station locations.

An unbounded quadratic helper solves (J^T J/6+alpha D^T D/7)sigma=J^T a/6, where D is the adjacent-difference matrix. This helper is an explicitly labeled comparison only. The historical upstream CSgn comparison instead uses J^T J+alpha L with L=D^T D, hence a different relative scaling. Both comparisons remain in the notebook. The anomalous site8 is retained. Its residual and the inadequacy of a locally layered approximation are discussed rather than hidden by removing the observation. Neither the standard image nor any comparison is ground truth.

NCC is the flattened uncentered cosine similarity. NRMSE is RMS image error divided by max(reference)-min(reference). No spatial/gain alignment, clipping, masks or rescaling are used. All standard computations and stored arrays use float64. A separate forward-consistency diagnostic reports every coil's residual without pretending an unavailable noise covariance is known.
