## Temporal analytic signal and local modes

For each record, dt is its stored average sample interval. Native window constants are60s and90s, starts spaced by int(30/dt) samples, maximum-window length nmax=int(90/dt)+1, extension nh=int(15/dt)+1. Only starts with the entire maximum window and both extensions plus native endpoint margin are admitted. Both records have nmax169, nh29, step56, and valid starts0,56; their start times are their own time[start+nh]. Extension is part of temporal conditioning, not an easier cropped benchmark: every admitted native window is reconstructed.

The analytic signal of an extended temporal row is FFT^-1(h FFT(x)), where h is1 at DC,2 at positive frequencies,0 at negative frequencies, and1 at the Nyquist bin for even lengths. After cropping nh samples from both ends, use the first min(int(window_seconds/dt),nmax) columns. This preserves native off-by-one conventions. With successive complex snapshots X0 and X1, compute X0=U S V*. Retain rank6. The reduced DMD matrix is U* X1 V S^-1 with inverse singular cutoff1e-14. Its eigenpairs give exact DMD spatial modes Phi=X1 V S^-1 W. For an eigenvalue eta, period T=2pi dt/Im(log eta), omega=2pi/T, spatial phase=arg Phi and amplitude=abs Phi. Retain finite periods3.5..15s. Full singular spectra remain diagnostic, not additional fitted outputs. Native rounded period/window filename keys determine any collisions and sorted processing order. Phase origin is arbitrary; parity cancels global phase rather than comparing a gauge-dependent origin.

## Adaptive spatial phase fitting

For a mode with period T, trial depths are0.5+j(6.0-0.5)/3m, j1,2,3, visited in descending order. For each, obtain k from gravity-wave dispersion and choose RK=0.6(2pi/k)m. These are wavelength-based configured neighborhoods, not the paper's generic approximate0.5 factor. For each K-grid site, collect M-grid samples within RK. Require at least6 and centroid no farther than RK/4 from the K point. Define coordinates relative to the closest M point and principal phase differences angle(exp(i(phase-phase0))). Fit phase=kx dx+ky dy+intercept. The vector is called a phase gradient; its sign is not asserted to be physical wave propagation direction.

RANSAC draws uniform ordered triples without replacement using the native sequential RandomState.choice(range(n),size=3,replace=False) convention. Solve each3-point plane through its normal equations, skip singular proposals without advancing the valid-trial counter, and count residuals<=0.25rad. Keep the first strictly largest consensus. With inlier fraction f, conservative outlier probability is1-f+0.2f. The required trial count is ceil(log(1-pDesired)/log(1-(1-outlier)^3)), clipped probabilities to[1e-12,1-1e-12], pDesired=1-1e-9, cap200; stop before incrementing the counter when every point is an inlier. Refit all consensus points by normal equations and require at least6 inliers for a valid wavevector. Actual random draw count and the source's valid-trial counter are distinct diagnostics. A20000-draw guard rejects pathological all-singular sampling rather than looping forever; it is inactive on qualified real cases.

The initial vectorized Generator proposal-prefetch implementation failed the frozen fixed-interior coverage gate despite passing local sampling-distribution tests. That first full result is preserved as failed evidence. The selected shipped method repairs fidelity by retaining native sequential RandomState sampling, no prefetch/discard of unused triples, unchanged seed20261026, same source ordering/parameters and arithmetic. No seed search, missing-point fill or threshold change was used. An independent32-call continuous-stream audit captured every original/local triple, coefficients, inliers, actual draw counts and complete post-call MT19937 state: all equal. Original uninterrupted K+B qualification of this exact fixed stream precedes a judged corrected full local run; see test/validation.md for actual gate status and logs. Seed is a reproducible method choice, not hidden acquisition information. Independent repeated distributions remain relevant to variability, not a substitute for that stream-fidelity check.

Set k=hypot(kx,ky) in rad/m and gamma=omega^2/(G k). Reject k<1e-6 or gamma>1.2; do not silently strengthen the noisy-data cutoff to gamma<=1. At each accepted K site/radius, compute mean and population standard deviation of gamma over accepted K sites within max(0.5(2pi/k),2.1deltaK), with deltaK5m and at least3 neighbors. Thus the radius floor is10.5m. Keep dispersion observations only where gamma SD<=0.075 and abs(gamma-mean_gamma)<=0.075. All decisions depend on the optical data, never survey error. Combine tuples of x,y,gamma,mean_gamma,omega and known recording water level from every retained mode, radius, window and both records. Native aggregation order is planview then oblique, with sorted mode keys and large-to-small radius columns, distinct from oblique-first processing order.

## Bed elevation fitting and validity

The exact relation is omega^2=G k tanh(kd), d=water_level-bed>0. Native explicit dispersion uses kappa=omega^2 d/G, mu=kappa/sqrt(tanh(kappa)) times [1+kappa^1.09 exp(-(1.56+1.28kappa+0.219kappa^2+0.00371kappa^3))], and k=mu/d. Source approximation accuracy is assessed against an independently solved exact root, preserving the original approximation rather than changing its target. Its relative-k error criterion0.0005 is different from the derived relative dispersion-equation residual bound0.00100025.

For each B-grid site, find tuples within1.1 times the nearest tuple distance plus1e-3m. Their characteristic wavelengths2pi G mean_gamma/omega^2 are averaged to LB. Use RB=max(0.2LB,2.1deltaK), retain nearby tuples, require at least10. Scan trial bed elevations at0.05m intervals from min(water_level)-6 to max(water_level)-0.5, upper endpoint excluded. Compute predicted gamma from the known water levels and dispersion; candidates violating minimum depth0.5m are ineligible. Choose the first strict maximum consensus with abs(observed_gamma-predicted_gamma)<0.075 and at least10 tuples. On that fixed consensus, minimize RMS gamma discrepancy by SciPy general unconstrained BFGS with native finite-difference convention. In this objective only, depth is safeguarded at1e-14 to match the source; there is no unannounced constrained refit or survey-calibrated offset. The scalar gamma residual and consensus size are diagnostics.

The actual shared SciPy1.17.1 scalar BFGS defaults were inspected directly. Initial bed is the first maximal-consensus0.05m grid candidate above; the inverse-Hessian estimate starts at1. With no supplied Jacobian, the objective gradient uses an absolute forward difference eps=sqrt(float64 machine epsilon)=1.4901161193847656e-8m. The search direction is minus the current inverse-Hessian estimate times that gradient. A strong-Wolfe line search uses c1=1e-4 and c2=0.9; BFGS updates the inverse-Hessian estimate from the accepted position/gradient differences. Stop when the gradient infinity norm is<=1e-5, or the native numerical/failure conditions occur; default maximum iterations is200 times dimension, hence200 here. xrtol=0 disables a positive relative-position convergence tolerance. These defaults are implementation conventions of the selected scalar solver, not acquisition parameters or calibrated error tolerances. On precision loss or another unsuccessful flag, the native returned position remains the method estimate and the flag is explicitly reported, not relabeled as certified convergence.

A first raw bed can exist where final validity fails. Native spatial bed SD is the population standard deviation of raw neighboring bed estimates within RB, requiring at least3 geometric B neighbors and retaining ordinary std rather than nanstd. Any missing neighboring raw bed propagates a missing SD. Final bed is invalid wherever this SD is missing. This erosion is part of the selected source method, not a post-fit survey-error mask. Output uses float64 reconstruction(1,8963), bool validity(1,8963); invalid reconstruction is exactly0 as a storage sentinel, never a physical zero elevation. Local spatial SD is not a posterior uncertainty or calibrated confidence interval. Native BFGS returned estimates are retained even when its success flag reports precision loss, as in the source; optimizer_success/evaluation count/gamma RMS are diagnostic and no all-converged assertion is made. Native text reports round bed and SD to4decimals; the NPZ retains float64 fitted precision. Deterministic scalar parity is checked before formatting, while archived native map comparisons include their<=5e-5m report quantization.
