# Implemented mathematical approach

The task fits the author's entire bound compilation (2964 intensity,4253 inclination,2827 declination values, at1725 locations). Dates run from−2000 through1990 CE. Inputs are grouped F,I,D with their own rows, locations, times, and uncertainties; direction values and uncertainties are converted from degrees to radians. Dates are treated as exact by the selected algorithm. This omits age uncertainty, as does this upstream run.

The magnetic potential is V=a Σ(n=1..3) Σ(m=0..n)(a/r)^(n+1)[g cos(mφ)+h sin(mφ)]Pbar(cosθ), with reference radius a=6371.2km. Pbar is Schmidt seminormalized, with no Condon–Shortley phase. Spatial coefficient ordering is g10,g11,h11,g20,g21,h21,g22,h22,g30,g31,h31,g32,h32,g33,h33. All coefficients are nT. North/east/down geocentric field follows (∂θV/r,−∂φV/(r sinθ),∂rV), including radial factors(a/r)^(n+2). This exponent corrects a verified upstream code inconsistency with its paper Eq.3; see validation.

For geodetic latitude λ and height h=0m, use major/minor semiaxes A=6378137m and C=6356752m. Set flattening f=(A−C)/A and eccentricity squared e²=f(2−f). The prime-vertical radius is N=A/sqrt(1−e²sin²λ). Set u=e²N/(N+h), geocentric latitude λc=atan[(1−u)tanλ], and radius r=(N+h)sqrt[1−u(2−u)sin²λ]/1000km. Rotate geocentric north and down into geodetic components with cd=cos(λ−λc), sd=sin(λ−λc): Xg=Xc cd+Zc sd, Zg=Zc cd−Xc sd; Y is unchanged. This also corrects the upstream flattening denominator, while retaining the supplied rounded semiaxes.

For each coefficient, g(t)=Σ(k=0..42) c[k]B[k](t), with ordinary cubic B-spline basis B, degree3, on knots−2300:100:2300 CE. There are43 splines and15 spatial coefficients, hence645 inverse unknowns. This is a deliberately low-degree benchmark of the same configurable RLS method, not the paper's historical degree10/ten-year demonstration. All bound measurements remain active. Maps are evaluated at five interior epochs (−1500,−500,500,1000,1700 CE) to reduce endpoint artefacts; coefficients themselves include the full time support.

With p=(X,Y,Z,H,F,I,D), H=sqrt(X²+Y²), F=sqrt(H²+Z²), I=asin(Z/F), D=atan2(Y,X). Define residual e=d−p, wrapping declination into[−π,π]. The objective is Σ(e/σ)²+cᵀPc, without mean normalization. P=ζS+τT. Spatial S integrates B[k]B[l] over−2000..2000; temporal T integrates B''[k]B''[l] over the same interval. The common spectral radius ratio is q=6371.2/3485. For each degree n, spatial weight is4πq^(2n+3)(n+1)(2n+1)(2n+3)/n, except the three dipole terms are spatially exempt. Temporal weight is4πq^(2n+4)(n+1)²/(2n+1), including dipoles. These are the paper's spectral regularizers, with omitted material constants inherited from upstream; they are not SI heat fluxes. Damping strengths have the reciprocal model units needed to match the dimensionless data objective. The selected ζ=1e−13, τ=1e−3 match the actual independent bound-data run.

Piecewise Newton–Cotes quadrature integrates spline products exactly to polynomial degree6 (spatial) and2 (temporal). The interval overlap and endpoint convention match upstream integrator. Temporal interval100 years is included in the quadrature factor. Damping matrices are formed explicitly using a Kronecker product with degree weights.

The instantaneous Jacobians are dH=(X dX+Y dY)/H; dF=(H dH+Z dZ)/F; dI=(H dZ−Z dH)/F²; dD=(X dY−Y dX)/H². Multiply each by its temporal basis and divide byσ to form the whitened design J. Initialization sets all spline g10=−30000nT, with every other coefficient zero. Each full Gauss–Newton update solves (JᵀJ+P)δ=Jᵀ(e/σ)−Pc. Cholesky solves the positive-definite system. Apply δ with unit step; there is no line search, clipping, optimizer substitution, or implicit likelihood scaling. Stop after exactly five updates. The original banded assembly and cleaned dense assembly implement the same normal equations; this benchmark's645 unknowns make the explicit dense version small enough.

Diagnostics store mean absolute whitened residual before each update, matching the original run's reporting. It is MAE, not RMS or chi-square. Additional review plots compute true weighted RMS separately. Independent original/corrected measurements and fresh measurements remain separately labeled. The notebook also recomputes τ=1e−4,1e−3,1e−2; these sensitivity runs are comparisons, while the standard entry point uses τ=1e−3.

Evaluation compares all five epochs and three vector components separately with the corrected standard-method oracle, and also scores the complete43×15 spline matrix. NCC is uncentered cosine similarity; NRMSE is RMS error/reference dynamic range. No field scaling, magnitude conversion, crop, rotation, or alignment is applied. Numerical boundaries are NCC≥0.9999999 and NRMSE≤1e−7, justified by independently captured original/corrected runs and the observed~1e−15 relative extraction discrepancy. No physical truth is available; this measures numerical standard-method agreement, not geophysical uniqueness or recovery of unresolved features.

The standalone Judge also independently synthesizes F/I/D from submitted spline coefficients at every public observation. It requires separately whitened RMS≤1.7666134171053434 forintensity,≤1.8787429787863662 forinclination, and≤1.3698116303861663 fordeclination, exactly1.1 times the independently reproduced corrected-source residuals. Declination wraps in degrees; sigma is the public supplied uncertainty. This observation criterion supplements every reference-array criterion.
