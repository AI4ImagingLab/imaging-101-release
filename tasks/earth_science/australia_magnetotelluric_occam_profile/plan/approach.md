# Implemented mathematics and method

The inverse target is m(j)=log10(rho(j)/(1 ohm metre)) on 25 horizontally layered spatial cells; z(j) is the supplied layer-top depth in metres and h(j)=z(j+1)-z(j). The final layer is a half-space. The 23 periods T in seconds give omega=2pi/T; permeability mu=4pi 10^-7 H/m. The quasistatic propagation constant q(j)=sqrt(i omega mu / 10^m(j)) uses Re(q)>0. The complex transfer length in metres is C(L)=1/q(L) and C(j)=(q(j)C(j+1)+tanh(q(j)h(j)))/(q(j)(1+q(j)C(j+1)tanh(q(j)h(j)))). Surface observables are log10(omega mu |C(1)|²), dimensionless, and atan2(Re C(1),-Im C(1)), radians. No mean subtraction or phase alignment is used.

The stable identity tanh(u)=-expm1(-2u)/(1+exp(-2u)) avoids overflow in Octave's large complex tanh and evaluates the same forward model. Its numerator has good cancellation behavior for small u. The standard response routine uses the exact supplied permeability. In the original Occam driver's per-iteration initial response, a separately written rounded coefficient 7.8957e-6 is preserved verbatim in rho_a=7.8957e-6 |C|² f. This differs from 2pi mu by roughly 2.1e-6 relative and is explicitly retained for algorithm parity, not silently recomputed. Forward residual evaluation always uses the actual recursion routine and exact mu.

Let d contain concatenated observed log10 apparent resistivity and phase, let F(m) contain the two predicted channels, and W be diagonal inverse standard deviations. D is the 25-by-25 first-difference matrix with zero first row and subsequent rows m(j)-m(j-1). The smoothness penalty is ||Dm||². At a linearization m(k), define J=dF/dm and translated observation d-F(m(k))+Jm(k). The update solves

```math
m^{\mathrm{next}}=(\lambda D^T D+J^T W^2 J)^{-1}
J^T W^2[d-F(m^k)+Jm^k].
```

This is the minimizer of 1/2||W(Jm-[d-F(m(k))+Jm(k)])||² + lambda/2||Dm||². All concatenated data are used, and reported standardized RMS divides the sum of their squared residuals by 46. J has 46 rows and 25 columns; the channel-specific code keeps its two 23-row blocks. The linear system is solved directly with NumPy; no domain inverse solver is called.

The source derivative recurrence first forms dC/drho in linear ohm metre units. The logarithmic resistivity Jacobian J1=2 rho Re(conj(C) dC/drho)/|C|² cancels the log(10) from log10(rho_a). Phase has no such cancellation: J2=log(10) rho [Re(C) Im(dC/drho)-Im(C) Re(dC/drho)]/|C|². The archived original missed log(10)rho in J2; this task implements the independently verified correction. This is a corrected implementation of the original intended Occam mathematics, not an unchanged reproduction of the defective code.

Initialize every m(j)=2 (100 ohm metre), lambda=10. For each of at most 20 nonlinear iterations, evaluate candidate next models at lambda/10, lambda, and 10lambda. Select the middle candidate if it has the least chi-square; otherwise shift the bracket by one decade toward the smaller chi-square. Permit at most ten such searches. Lower lambda search stops before lambda/10 <=0.0001; upper stops when 10lambda>=100000. Retain upstream exact inequalities and candidate updates. If the chosen model worsens standardized RMS compared with the preceding model, retain it but reduce lambda by 100 and continue, matching the upstream restart convention.

Once standardized RMS reaches target 1.0, increase lambda by sqrt(10) repeatedly. Recompute the residual at the accepted iterate, retaining the current iteration Jacobian as the original driver does. Accept smoother candidates strictly below target, and stop at the first one above target. No extra tolerance, interpolation, or monotonicity heuristic is introduced. The bound case reaches target on its second nonlinear iteration and accepts one smoothing candidate. The notebook's target-zero runs suppress target smoothing to expose six actual nonlinear updates; target .7/1/1.5/2 runs are genuine sensitivity analyses, not the standard submission path.

Evaluation requires float64 (1,25) log10 resistivity on exactly the same grid. The baseline is the corrected original method result; actual geological truth is unavailable. NCC is cosine similarity; NRMSE is RMS profile difference divided by baseline dynamic range. Nonzero numerical tolerance is measured against independent Octave reruns and the NumPy port. The independent Judge also evaluates both observation channels and requires standardized RMS<=1.0. Reference similarity is a numerical reproducibility test, not a physical uniqueness claim. There is no hidden seed, privileged target calibration, synthetic generator, or alignment.
