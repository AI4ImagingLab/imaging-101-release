## Coordinate investigation and independent physics

The paper main Eqs4/5 describe named F as difference-angle and R as sum-angle, while appendix Eq9/Eqs17–20 and actual source map F to sum-angle and R to difference-angle in the stored raw numeric angle coordinates. Both archived paper versions have this discrepancy. P/independent_convention_audit.py independently expands both expressions, with no upstream/local reconstruction import. Identifying paper illumination angle with minus the stored generator angle reconciles the two equations to4.44e−16. The metadata does not independently establish mechanical viewing handedness, so that identification is documented as a convention reconciliation, not a discovered physical hardware calibration.

Independent Jones tests use a single mirror diag(1,−1) and two successive mirrors in declared local propagation bases. They reproduce cos²(c+g) and cos²(c−g) to2.22e−16. A fully depolarized input gives the constant component. These checks validate the angular-family algebra and show why viewing direction matters. They do not certify that every real fitted component has a unique reflection order. Public output explicitly names both harmonic families and retains source F/R mapping in review plots/provenance.

Separate analytic Malus-law mixtures test all five recovered fields, exact field/coefficient reversibility, total angular-mean power and positive radiometric unit scaling. A generator-angle finite difference independently checks the opposite signs of difference-angle derivatives. Frame permutation preserves the answer; rank-deficient acquisition fails explicitly. Negative inferred U is preserved. The full original W and full9basis design both have condition2; no numerical degeneracy is hidden.
