# Implemented mathematics

The selected method is the original paper repository's classical second-order spatial-derivative baseline (called TGV upstream), not its learned variational network. Its actual penalty is the pixelwise complex-vector L2 norm of (Dxx q,Dyy q). It is neither auxiliary-field Bredies TGV nor the L1 norm of the summed Laplacian. LN and first-order TV are active scientific comparisons. No learned weights are published/bundled in this task and no network result is represented as reproduced.

Use exp(+i omega t), omega=2*pi*1150 s^-1, k=omega/343 m^-1, rho=1.2 kg/m³. Receivers are inclusive linspace x=[0,.825] (12), y=[0,.6] (9), z=.025 m. Source points use 50 x and 40 y samples on the same rectangle, z=0. Row-major flattening gives G shape108x2000. G[m,j]=i*omega*rho*exp(-i*k*r)/(4*pi*r), r the Euclidean distance in m. G has units Pa s/m³. The input pressure has composed author scale U; q units U m³/(Pa s). No area factor, conjugation of pressure, fitted gain, phase alignment or input normalization is introduced.

LN minimizes ||Gq-p||²+lambda||q||². With G=U diag(s) V^H it evaluates V diag(s/(s²+lambda)) U^H p. For TV/TGV the objective is ||Gq-p||²+lambda sum_pixels sqrt(abs(Dx^n q)²+abs(Dy^n q)²), n=1/2. These derivatives are in grid index units, with no division by physical pixel spacing, preserving the source discretization. Forward differences use right/bottom zero padding. For n=2, Dxx q[x]=q[x+2]-2q[x+1]+q[x], and Dyy is the same along rows. The implemented adjoint includes the original zero-edge/corner convention and passes an independent complex128 inner-product check. Do not substitute periodic boundaries.

Let smax be the largest singular value of G, N=2000, and a=||p||. The source parameter is lambda=smax*10^(-SNR/20)*a/sqrt(N). SNR is a regularization-tuning label, not an independently known acquisition noise value. LN uses30dB, TV/TGV35dB. The squared-error gradient is 2G^H(Gq-p). Set tau=.5/smax², beta=2smax², eps=matrix spectral norm of D*D(I100), sigma=(1/tau-beta/2)/eps. This particular I100 estimate is retained from the original implementation rather than silently replaced with a different operator bound.

Initialize q complex64 zero and dual u float32 zero (becomes complex after first update). For exactly20000 iterations: qnew=q-tau*2G^H(Gq-p)-tau*D*u; unew=projection_radius_lambda(u+sigma*D(2qnew-q)). Projection divides each two-component complex vector by max(1,its_L2_norm/lambda). Original relaxation rho=1 and fused sparsity mu=0 are identity operations. No line search or early stopping is used. LN is closed form. CPU torch operations run with one thread. Complex64 and source floating-point operation order are preserved. Diagnostic checkpoints do not change iteration updates.

Hidden references are method outputs, never ground truth. Exact complex scale is fixed by public p and the selected discretization. Judge requires relative complex L2<=2e-5, magnitude cosine>=.999999, and independent complex128 pressure relative residual<=.03188075004145503 (1.05 times the original residual). The nonzero parity tolerance was fixed from original stage/full-run conventions; agreement is reproducibility rather than proof of physical truth. Comparisons show the measured residuals and regularization dependence; multiple equivalent sources can fit the same pressure.
