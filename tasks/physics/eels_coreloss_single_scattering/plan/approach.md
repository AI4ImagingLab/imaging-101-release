# Implemented mathematics

This is a local extraction of released eXSpy0.3.2 Fourier-ratio deconvolution, with the author's valid list API used to align the paired core-loss energy axis. It preserves both measured count arrays unchanged. `solve(low,core)` requires 2048-channel float64 vectors and returns a dictionary of fresh numerical stages.

The visible low-loss maximum is channel204, nominal+0.8eV. Subtract this from both nominal axes, producing origins-40.8 and359.2eV. All grids have h=.2eV. The nearest-index convention is NumPy ties-to-even rounding. Fit the two-area background on corrected600–638eV; the upper index is exclusive and parity adjusted. With i,j endpoints, m=(i+j)/2, U1=sum(y[i:m]), U2=sum(y[m:j]), r=2(logU1-logU2)/(log x[j]-log x[i]), k=1-r and A=kU2/(x[j]^k-x[m]^k). The background in binned counts is .2*A*x**(-r). Subtract on the full core grid and crop at600eV to844channels.

Repeat this two-area fit over the original low-loss final100channels or cropped core final20channels, from index n-w to n-1, preserving original code's exclusive/parity convention. Append100model bins. Do not clip negative exponents: original `fix_neg_r=False`. Apply Hann halves with q=max(20,round(.02*n)) separately to each extended array. Finite arrays are padded right to next_fast_len(2148+844-1)=3000, preserving original code's use of original core length rather than its extended length in this sizing expression.

The original low-loss derivative over[1,11)eV uses Savitzky–Golay length5,degree3,firstderivative,delta.2,edge polynomial interpolation. T is its firstminimum absolute derivative. I0 is sum of the tapered extended low-loss bins preceding rounded T. It is binned counts, not a trapezoid and not multiplied byh. A zero-smoothing not-a-knot cubic spline of taperedextendedlow−max/2 gives twohalfmaximum roots; W is theirseparation, sigma=W/2.3548. Sample G=exp(-x²/(2sigma²))/(sqrt(2pi)*sigma) on the length3000 lowlossaxis, density1/eV. No Gaussian h factor appears in this source normalization.

Evaluate S_full=I0*irfft(rfft(G)*rfft(V)/rfft(L),n=3000). The benchmarkoutput is its first844entries, counts/eV. This is a closed-form inverse: no initialization, iteration, optimizer, learnedweights or stoppingrule exists. No clipping or rescaling is applied. The convolution identity uses discrete sums, and is checked on the full3000domain; croppedvalues alone do not support a full convolution residual.

Stage tolerances rtol1e-9,atol1e-8 and pipeline dynamicrangeNRMSE1e-8 were frozen after exactupstreamsuccess and beforelocal extraction. Public Judge boundaries are1e-5 dynamicrangeNRMSE and1e-5 relativeintegralerror, acceptingfloat32 and avoiding exactalgorithm identity. No true unbroadened spectrum is available. The hidden reference is explicitly a validated standardmethod output at the declaredGaussianresolution and specified finitewindowprotocol.
