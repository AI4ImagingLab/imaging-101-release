# Implemented resonator inference

The complete 1001-point real sweep is retained in Hz and complex dimensionless voltage ratio, float64/complex128. No grid generation, interpolation, target-derived calibration or synthetic observations are used. The forward response is

```math
S(f)=a e^{i\alpha-2\pi if\tau}\left(1-\frac{Q_l e^{i\phi}}{|Q_c|(1+2iQ_l(f-f_r)/f_r)}\right).
```

f and fr are frequency in Hz, tau is seconds, a is dimensionless positive gain, alpha and phi are phases in radians, Ql is loaded quality and |Qc| the magnitude of complex coupling quality. Complex coupling is |Qc| exp(-i phi); corrected Qc=|Qc|/cos(phi), Qi=(1/Ql-1/Qc)^-1. These are the original notch conventions. The physical model is an isolated linear resonance with constant gain and linear cable phase across the observed band. There is no regularizer, training, clipping, background subtraction, crop or amplitude-only replacement.

Initialization fits measured squared magnitude to a skewed Lorentzian with constant and linear background and numerator skew. Initial fr is the measured minimum, constant amplitude is the average endpoint power, and the half-depth width initializes Ql (falling back to 1000). Leastsq fits slope, numerator skew and Ql with ftol=xtol=1e-12, followed by bounded curve_fit of all six parameters. Frequency is bounded by the sweep and Ql by zero; exceptions retain the first-stage values. The baseline slope is explicitly set to zero, as in the upstream default. This preserves the author method and does not claim to remove the visible residual slope.

Cable delay starts from a linear regression of unwrapped scattering phase. Each proposed delay rotates observations by exp(2 pi i f tau). An algebraic circle fit forms the quartic polynomial of centered coordinate moments, uses fsolve initialized at zero with the polynomial derivative and SciPy defaults, and recovers circle center/radius from the smallest-singular-value vector of the constrained moment matrix. Optional radial iterative refinements remain explicit source functions; the bound default disables them. Leastsq varies delay to minimize radial circle deviations, retaining original maxfev=0 convention (SciPy default evaluations). No fixed instrumental calibration is read from a reference.

After delay correction, center the fitted circle and fit its phase to theta+2 arctan(2 Ql (1-f/fr)). Periodic phase distance folds residuals across pi; frequency and Q are normalized by their scale to avoid ill conditioning. Five staged leastsq fits successively fit phase/frequency, Q/frequency, frequency, Q, and finally phase/Q/frequency. Each stage uses ftol=xtol=1e-12 and SciPy default evaluation limits. Circle off-resonance geometry supplies a and alpha. Normalize measured observations by a exp(i alpha-2 pi i f tau), then fit the normalized circle and phase again.

The final circle radius r gives |Qc|=Ql/(2r), and its center determines phi=-arcsin(ycenter/r). Apply diameter correction as above. Covariance uses the explicit task-specific complex resonance Jacobian and residual normalization by degrees of freedom; correlated uncertainty propagation supplies internal-Q error. It is conditional least-squares covariance, not calibrated metrological uncertainty. All original updates, factors and uncertainty formulas remain executable in the local extracted functions. The independent forward implementation checks the complex sign and loss relation.

The original-method reference is not truth. Evaluation compares resonance relative error, maximum relative error among all three Q values, full complex response relative L2 error, and normalized raw observation residual. Tolerances were fixed in the private case before extraction; observation bound is 1.1 times original residual 0.00879655899647674. There is no alignment, magnitude conversion or gain fitting in Judge. The independent rerun and extracted code coincide numerically on this CPU; nonzero tolerance allows numerical changes without defining zero self-error as an acceptance threshold.
