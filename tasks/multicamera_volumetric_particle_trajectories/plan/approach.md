# Implemented mathematics and conventions

## Scope and qualification

The selected current author tracer pipeline processes all400 existing simulated native images,100frames, four cameras and the complete[-20,20]³mm domain. It is not the source's bubble path, backward tracking, optional VSC or the2020 table configuration. VSC is explicitly disabled in the original current path. One independently proven source correction resets the trial particle centre from the accepted full centre at each coordinate-axis entry. Original unchanged results remain separate. The exact-input state probe finds a stale nonactive coordinate differing by0.01mm; four independent known-particle cases verify all48 qualified axis and16 full updates do not increase the actual objective.

## Coordinates, detection and calibration

Perspective/distortion equations are defined in README. Ray inversion iteratively undistorts normalized coordinates with50 iterations maximum and1e-5pixel convergence; it uses stored algebraic inverse R and stored origin C, not R transpose. Unit direction is normalized explicitly. Least-square triangulation sums projectors Q=I-ddᵀ and solves (ΣQ)r=ΣQC through the original scaled-pivot3×3 inverse. Ray-distance error retains the source cancellation convention sqrt(||r-C||²-[(r-C)·d]²), with tiny negative values within1e-8 clipped to zero and larger negative values rejected.

Detection scans row-major interior pixels at threshold30ADU. Up/left neighbours reject strictly greater values; down/right reject greater-or-equal values. This preserves actual executable ties despite the opposite upstream comment. The two coordinates use direct three-point log-Gaussian peak formula; log floor1e-4ADU. No image interpolation or crop occurs.

Fresh uniform OTF uses first five frames per camera, with at most1000 detected particles/frame sampled using original GCC13 default_random_engine/minstd_rand0 seed123 and paired Fisher-Yates draw sequence. This is observation-derived calibration, not hidden-target information. Peak A=min(mean peak+2population standard deviations,max peak). All pixels from floor(u-2)..ceil(u+3), and similarly v, provide the fit -log(max(I,1e-4))+log(A)=B(x-u)²+C(y-v)². Explicit2×2 least squares estimates B,C. Uniform5³ grid interpolation retains original sequential trilinear arithmetic and alpha0. No fitted peak is publicly supplied.

## Stereo competition

Use the active camera with fewest detections as reference. Epipolar strips of halfwidth0.8pixel, then1.2pixel, constrain candidates. Four-pixel indexing cells accelerate lookup without changing the final exact distance/backprojection tests. Camera ordering uses projected ray length and angular spread. Intermediate triangulation tolerance scales0.04sqrt(m/k) with clipped parallax factor sin6°/sin(max(angle,1°)) in[1,2], where m is total active camera count and k current rays. Final RMS ray distance must be≤0.04mm and the point inside the full domain.

Candidate ID tuples are sorted lexicographically. The selected modern source computes tied percentile ranks among competitors for each image detection, averages them over cameras, and greedily enforces exclusive image-detection ownership with higher-quality replacement. Relative tie tolerance1e-9, absolute1e-12, replacement margin1e-12. This differs from the paper's integer preference count and is stated explicitly. Full candidate/selected fixtures validate actual convention.

## Projection residual and coordinate refinement

For each particle, the Gaussian projection uses Aexp[-Bdx²-Cdy²]. Projection support uses a4×4pixel rounded rectangle centred as round(u-(width-1)/2), not an implicit symmetric crop. Augmented support is8×8pixels. Residual fusion is the original minimum-over-single-particle subtraction min(current,I-Pj), not subtraction of a sum. Normal refinement residuals may be negative; IPR output residuals clamp to zero. These are the selected original overlap approximation and do not claim exact superposition fitting.

At each of four outer shake iterations, rebuild all residuals from the current particle set (Jacobi baseline). For each particle, fixed augmented observations are max(0,residual+own projection within original4×4support). The objective is arithmetic mean of per-active-camera pixel MSE, with model predicted only within floor(u-2)..ceil(u+3). At least two usable cameras are needed. Initial shake halfwidth0.01mm halves per iteration to0.005,0.0025,0.00125, clamped below by0.0005.

For each XYZ axis, reset trial centre to accepted centre, evaluate offsets -Δ,0,+Δ. Curvature κ=f(-Δ)-2f(0)+f(+Δ), slope g=[f(+Δ)-f(-Δ)]/max(2Δ,1e-12). If κ>0 and parabolic vertex lies within0.95Δ, skip expansion; predicted relative gain g²/[2κ/(Δ²+1e-12)max(f(0),1)] below8e-4 also skips vertex evaluation. Otherwise, monotonicity exceeding1e-3max(1,f(0)) may request a single expansion. Median projection derivative J and median ROI pixel margin limit its world step to half-margin/J and one-pixel scale, or1.15 times an exterior convex vertex. Explicit normal-matrix inverse fits a quadratic to all evaluated candidates, preserving (XᵀX)^-1 Xᵀ y grouping. A convex fitted vertex is evaluated only within evaluated bounds and all selected ROIs. Strictly lowest evaluated cost wins, first tie retained.

Tracer selection flags are true for all four camera slots even in a reduced-camera pass. Inactive empty ROIs therefore veto optional expansion/vertex evaluations; active-camera cost still excludes them. This unusual source convention is preserved, not silently improved. After each outer iteration score multiplies sqrt(clamp(sum measured/sum modeled,2/3,3/2)), excluding the active camera with largest measured peak when more than two exist. Repeated points within0.04mm are marked; scores below0.1 times nonrepeated mean, or zero, are ghost flags. All flags are retained in parity cases; only native flagged candidates are removed by the selected algorithm.

## IPR and trajectories

Perform two full-camera iterations, then two each on camera subsets012,013,023,123. Tolerance starts0.8pixel for each subset and multiplies1.5 per iteration. At most200000 detected points per camera are sampled with original seed1234 if needed; this cap is inactive here. Residuals are updated after every accepted particle group, including all four views even for reduced-camera passes.

For initial four frames, correlate particle spaces on51³nodes spaced0.8mm. Each node uses particles in its strict coordinate cube±1mm. All current-minus-previous pair displacements contribute to an11³bin Gaussian histogram with bin coordinate2.5d+5, support±one bin around rounded centre. Select global peak above0.1 and fit three log-Gaussian sub-bin peaks. The field is smoothed componentwise by separable sigma1node/radius3Gaussian with zero continuation and no edge renormalization, then trilinearly sampled. Nearest particles within0.4mm extend initial short tracks; native initialization permits different tracks to select one point. Four-point tracks become active long tracks.

Established tracks use last at most six positions and order≤5 adaptive LMS. If the last coordinate magnitude<1, add10 to that coordinate history. Starting filter0 and error equal the last value, step is reciprocal energy of prior history, update w←w+step*h*error until absolute error≤1e-8 or5000iterations. Next estimate uses history shifted by one; zero-energy fallback is last+(last-previous). Remove shift10 after predicting. These are explicit LMS iterations, not a polynomial-fit substitute.

Predictions outside domain or visible in fewer than two cameras exit; only segments of length≥7 are retained as exited/inactive. Valid predictions are refined by the same shake routine and append at current frame. Reconstruct new particles from residuals and reject overlap≤0.04mm with active last points. Short-track association estimates velocity from nearby long tracks with weights1/(1+d²/L²), L²=clamp(0.64mean(d²),(0.1R)²,(0.8R)²), R=1.2mm×1.1^step and neighbor band3R. Five expanding attempts find nearest new particle within R. Conflicting short-track claims retain lowest predicted squared distance. At four points promote to long. All long tracks pass a final-four-point linear regression check, last-point error≤0.05mm; otherwise retain inactive only if length≥7. Unlinked short tracks are dropped by the original algorithm, not a postfit evaluation mask. Final categories are concatenated with distinct segment namespaces.
