# Implemented three-band structural inference

The selected published path is independent Gaussian maximum-a-posteriori Sérsic fitting using the original package's PixelRenderer, with a constant residual sky in each band. All three measured g/r/z observations are fitted; unknowns are extended intrinsic brightness and eight physical parameters per band. This is not point-source flux inference, posterior sampling, a trained network, or a joint wavelength-constrained fit. The original author's fixed 120x120 central region of the 256x256 DECaLS cutout is retained at native 0.262 arcsec sampling and float32 precision. The original PSFs and inverse variances are acquisition calibration. A public observed r-band segmentation mask excludes other sources, retains the source at original ROI pixel(60,60), and is shared across bands. No hidden target creates the mask or calibration.

## Profile, normalization and coordinates

Let p=(xc,yc,F,re,n,e,theta,s). Pixel columns x and rows y are zero-based centers0..119; +x points west and +y north. F is total infinite-plane flux in nanomaggies, re is major-axis half-light radius in pixels, n is dimensionless concentration, e=1−minor/major axis ratio, theta is radians in[0,pi), and s is constant sky flux in nanomaggies/pixel. One nanomaggy has AB magnitude22.5. The angle used in the rotation is beta=theta+pi/2. Set X=(x−xc)cos(beta)+(y−yc)sin(beta), Y=−(x−xc)sin(beta)+(y−yc)cos(beta), and z=sqrt[(X/re)^2+(Y/((1−e)re))^2].

The surface brightness is I=A exp[−bn(z^(1/n)−1)]/(1−e), with A=F bn^(2n)/[exp(bn+lgamma(2n)) re^2 pi 2n]. This normalization integrates to F over the infinite plane. The delivered finite grid is never rescaled to force its finite sum to F. The local code evaluates the original analytic half-light constant approximation directly:

bn=2n−1/3+4/(405n)+46/(25515n²)+H(n),

H(n)=[(0.00018073182821237496/(n+exp(n))+0.000037026973571904255)/(−0.09149183119702775−n)²] * [log(n)+(2.6248718397705195n−0.9727511612512357)/(n^(−4)+94.78011643586419)]/(n−0.006044236674273689).

It approximates the implicit half-light gamma relation, rather than replacing the original convention with a different special-function inverse. An independent continuous radial integral verifies total flux and the half-light fraction for the exponential case. Independent finite differences verify all eight objective derivatives, in addition to original automatic-gradient parity.

## Pixel integration and measured seeing

Outside the fixed central rows/columns54:66, evaluate I at pixel centers. In those central12x12pixels, replace center samples with a12by12tensor Gauss–Legendre integral. Obtain nodes and weights on[−1,1], divide each by2 for a unit pixel interval, evaluate I at each pixel center plus both nodes, and sum with product weights. This quadrature region is fixed at the image center, matching original PixelRenderer; it does not move with a hidden centroid. Smooth sky s is added after PSF convolution, not integrated into the galaxy image.

Convolve using rfft2 on the full120x120periodic grid. The calibrated63x63PSF is transformed with zero padding to the image size. Multiply by separate phase factors exp[i2*3.1415*31*fx] and exp[i2*3.1415*31*fy], where fx is the nonnegative real-FFT xfrequency and fy the signed yfrequency in cycles/pixel. The positive phase shifts the centered PSF back to the Fourier origin. Use the original literal3.1415, not silently exactpi: independently, the implied centering shift is31*(1−3.1415/pi)=approximately0.000914pixel per axis. This tiny source numeric approximation is explicitly quantified and lies below the structural evaluation tolerance. It is not called an exact identity. Full fixed-parameter original PSF transfer, intrinsic and convolved images are frozen and compared.

The observed model is irfft2[rfft2(intrinsic)*PSFtransfer]+s. The convolution is circular at the declared image boundary; the galaxy is well inside the field but faint outskirts and real coadd correlations remain limitations. An independent delta-PSF test checks total discrete convolution flux. Fourier sign, full physical flux and the original approximate PSF centering are all preserved.

## Likelihood, priors and unconstrained variables

For each unmasked pixel, supplied RMS is1/sqrt(inverse_variance), computed in the original float32 convention. Minimize the Gaussian negative log likelihood sum[.5((model−image)/RMS)²+log(RMS)+.5log(2pi)], plus .5[((xc−60)/5)²+((yc−60)/5)²+(s/.01)²]. Thus position priors are independent Normal(60,5pixels), sky Normal(0,.01nanomaggies/pixel). Uniform structural priors have fixed bounds F[0,300],re[.5,30],n[.65,8],e[0,.9],theta[0,pi]. These broad bounds were fixed before original execution, not estimated from hidden answers. Additive normal-prior constants are omitted because they do not change derivatives or MAP. No image-dependent normalization, fitted band gain or target regularization enters the objective.

Use eight unconstrained latent variables z. Positions are60+5z for the first two; the five bounded structural parameters are low+(high−low)*sigmoid(z); sky is.01*zlast. There is no change-of-variable Jacobian term in this MAP objective; the transformation enforces the original support, while physical uniform-prior density is constant. The original AutoDelta method likewise optimizes a point estimate of this physical model. It is not posterior-density sampling.

## Adam schedule, initialization and stopping

Use generic torch.optim.Adam, beta1=.9,beta2=.999,epsilon1e-8. The mathematical update is g=gradientJ, mt=.9 mt−1+.1g, vt=.999 vt−1+.001g², bias-correct both moments by their accumulated iteration counts, then z←z−learningrate*mhat/(sqrt(vhat)+epsilon). Gradients come from local explicit PyTorch mathematics, not a domain renderer. Moment states and global step count are restored with the best latent state at each new round, rather than reset. Three rounds use learning rates.05,.005,.0005, each capped at20000updates. Strictly lower float32 loss stores the post-update latent and optimizer state. On non-improvement increment a counter; stop when the previous count is already250, matching original patience convention. Each round restarts from the best prior round state and continues the same moment state; only rounds1/2 reset best comparison to infinity. Every actual loss is returned for review; no fabricated convergence curve.

Original AutoDelta initializes from seeded medians of pure prior draws. The local standard path preserves each exact optimizer-unconstrained initialization captured independently with original SVI.init at seeds20261020/21/22, before any optimizer update or likelihood-derived fitting. INITIAL_LATENT contains these24float32 constants, in the declared parameter order. The source Normal-base position/sky coordinates and logistic Uniform-base coordinates match local latent transforms. These are target-independent initialization constants, not answer-derived calibration. Physical starting centroids are g(55.57784271,60.52440643),r(55.79391861,57.93099594),z(55.74378586,59.65951538). Before round0, perform exactly one warm Adam update at.05, retaining its pre-update loss with its post-update latent/moments/global step as the initial best state. Round0 does not reset that best loss; rounds1/2 reset it to infinity.
