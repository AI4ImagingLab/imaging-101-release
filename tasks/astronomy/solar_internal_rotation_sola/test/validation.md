## Fixed task and scientific role

Recover the equator-symmetric two-dimensional solar cyclic-rotation field from the complete author-supplied oscillation-frequency-coefficient acquisition. The retained native instance has29617observations,101radial×51colatitude grid points,5151target locations and1000fully reorthogonalized Lanczos steps. ## Metrics, thresholds and strict Judge contract

The only required quantity is the entire `(1,101,51)` real float64 cyclic-rotation field in nHz; every point is scored. Maximum absolute discrepancy must be≤1e-3nHz. This nonzero field tolerance was fixed before extraction, then independently validated by original repeat and cross-backend local agreement. An additional observation-consistency criterion computes RMS((Kx-y)/reported_error) over all29617rows with the known public calibration. Native reference value1.19355437766235motivates boundary1.20. Native full-grid standard-method agreement plus this all-observation check prevents trivial constant/zero/changed-scale outputs.
