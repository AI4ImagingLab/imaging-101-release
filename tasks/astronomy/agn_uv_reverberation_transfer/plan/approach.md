Let continuum covariance k(t,t')=sigma^2 exp(-|t-t'|/tau). Here sigma is continuum variability amplitude and tau is damping time. Transfer response h_j(u)=a_j/w_j on[d_j-w_j/2,d_j+w_j/2], zero elsewhere; continuum has h_0=delta. The parameters d,w,a mean delay,width,integrated response scale. Covariance K_ij(t,t') is the double integral h_i(u)h_j(v)k(t-u,t'-v) du dv. Local physics_model evaluates explicit piecewise exponential antiderivatives for continuum/line, same-line and cross-line cases. Inside, replace the exponential/bracket product after sigma^2(a*tau/w)^2 by -2exp(-|D|/tau)+exp(-w/tau)[exp(-D/tau)+exp(D/tau)]+2(w-|D|)/tau. Cross-line integration uses the four shifted endpoint differences, including overlap terms; the extracted functions explicitly define them.
