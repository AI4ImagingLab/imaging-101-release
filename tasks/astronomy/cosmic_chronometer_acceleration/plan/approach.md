# Implemented mathematics

The data are thirty derived expansion rates y_i at dimensionless redshifts z_i, with independent standard errors e_i. The selected inverse recovers q=(1+z)H'/H-1 and H0=H(0). The observation equation follows by integrating d log H/dz=(1+q)/(1+z); it does not assert raw galaxy ages are supplied. All four kernels use a zero mean Gaussian process for H, not for q.

For amplitude a (km/s/Mpc), correlation length l (redshift), delta=x-y and r=abs(delta)/l, the squared exponential covariance is a² exp(-r²/2). The Matérn covariances are a² exp(-b r)P(r): for 5/2, b=sqrt(5), P=1+sqrt(5)r+5r²/3; for 7/2, b=sqrt(7), P=1+sqrt(7)r+14r²/5+7sqrt(7)r³/15; for 9/2, b=3, P=1+3r+27r²/7+18r³/7+27r⁴/35. These retain the original normalization and exact analytic derivatives. K01 differentiates the second argument and K11 both arguments, including their signs at equal redshift. Let g=a² exp(-br)(P'-bP); then K01=-sign(delta)g/l, K11=-a² exp(-br)(P''-2bP'+b²P)/l². The diagonal derivative prior variance is finite for every selected kernel.

The observation covariance A=K+diag(e²) has Cholesky factor L. Alpha=L^-T L^-1 y. The minimized negative log marginal likelihood is y^T alpha/2 + sum log diag(L) + N log(2pi)/2, without normalization by N or regularization. Its derivative is trace[(A^-1-alpha alpha^T)dK/dtheta]/2. Amplitude derivative is 2K/a; correlation length derivative is -r g/l. Initialization is [range(y)/2,range(z)/2]. Positive lower bounds are the initial values divided by 10^15. SciPy fmin_tnc uses the original default convergence and evaluation semantics and analytic gradient; only message output is silenced. Return codes0,1,2 indicate convergence; all other statuses raise. The primary initial-factor is1. Supplemental factors0.5 and2 change only the initial iterate, with original lower bounds retained.

On the fixed 200-point grid, cross-covariances k and d give posterior means H=k^T alpha and H'=d^T alpha. With v=L^-1 k, w=L^-1 d, the per-point variances are a²-sum(v²) and K11(0)-sum(w²); their covariance is -sum(vw), since the same-point stationary prior cross-covariance is zero. This yields four sets of 200 separate 2x2 marginals, not a complete covariance across redshift. No uncertainty clipping, physical positivity projection, prior on q, or covariance rescaling is applied.

The deceleration parameter is computed directly from H/H'. Its diagnostic delta variance is J C J^T with J=[-(1+z)H'/H²,(1+z)/H]. This local linearization and fitted-hyperparameter conditional covariance omit population systematics and hyperparameter uncertainty. They are not true physical confidence intervals. H0 is exactly the first grid sample. No simulated truth exists.

Fixed-hyperparameter original fixtures precede extraction and compare K, kernel derivatives, Cholesky, alpha, likelihood and likelihood gradient at rtol1e-10/atol1e-8. Independent central differences check all derivative signs and likelihood gradients, while analytic EdS, de Sitter and flat Lambda-CDM expansion laws check q. Final tolerances were frozen from twelve original fits (four kernels, three independent initial scales), not local error: q0.001 absolute, H1e-4 relative, H'0.001 marginal sigma, covariance0.001 marginal-sigma products; q and H0 identities1e-8 and1e-10. Original-only standardized observation RMS sets its boundary to1.1times the worst four-kernel source value. All required arrays and every point are scored. High-redshift kernel sensitivity is reported, not removed by a mask.
