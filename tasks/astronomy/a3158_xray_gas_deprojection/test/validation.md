## Physical interpretation and independent checks

The code's nhc=ne/nH and division by nhc make the emitted quantity nH², and its signed square root nH. Its original electron-density prose is inconsistent with its executable formula. We preserved values and correctly labeled hydrogen density. The XSPEC normalization uses DA²(1+z)²; DL=DA(1+z)² converts it to the source DL²/(1+z)² factor. The independent uniform sphere test analytically integrates cylinders, checks full spherical-shell volume, solves a known positive shell field, and checks the discrete transpose identity. A separate known-density test reconstructs its count-to-emission normalization from electron/hydrogen densities and angular-diameter distance, detecting an erroneous nhc factor.

The exterior fraction can exceed one in a noisy outer annulus; this is a preserved model subtraction, not a probability or clipped observation. Median-smoothed final standard profiles remain positive. The smoothing-off experiment returns negative outer density; the right relative-difference panel displays it, while negative values cannot appear on the left logarithmic axis. This limitation is visible and no alternative is substituted for standard output. Density and emissivity are separately smoothed and need not be exact squares.
