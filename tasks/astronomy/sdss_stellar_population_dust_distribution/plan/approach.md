## High-pass and attenuation stage

Normalize each template to median(y). On the ordered surviving wavelength samples (gaps are not filled), take the FFT. The original discrete window retains index0; rises over indices20..39 as(i+1)/20; uses the original reverse-index ramp at N-1-(40-i); sets indices40..N-41 to1, leaving other indices0. This ramp is not replaced with a symmetric ideal filter. Let h=real IFFT(FFT(f)*window), l=real IFFT(FFT(f)*(1-window)) with complementary DC restored to1. Return H(f)=[1+(h-median(h))/l]*median(h). Its scale is unchanged. H is nonlinear because of continuum division, so no false linear adjoint is asserted for this stage.

High-pass observed errors are constant median(s)/median(y)*median(H(y)). High-pass templates are again median-normalized. Fit the complete BIC/mixture algorithm below. With selected weights v, define b=v times normalized unfiltered templates and bh=v times normalized high-pass templates. Fine relative attenuation is f=y/b-H(y)/bh+1. The original rule sets nonfinite entries to1 and multiplies their post-fit stored high-pass errors by9999999999.9; this guard is inactive on the final supported binding. ## Explicit population search, in both stages

Define standardized residual basis Cji=(Bji-y_i)/s_i. For candidate weights w, residual r=wC. Initialize all198 single-template weight vectors. Each initial residual is iteratively clipped at mean±3 population-standard-deviations, retaining inclusive endpoints until no elements change; its initial objective is the sum of surviving r². Choose the strict-interior clipping mask of the best initial candidate for later nonzero-branch objectives. The initial objective masks therefore differ; do not interpret all candidates as a common Gaussian posterior.

At iteration t=1..10, snapshot all retained candidates and their minimum objective cmin. For every parent w and every template unit vector ej, form (t*w+ej)/(sum(t*w)+1). Evaluate on the current fixed mask; retain a child only if its objective <cmin-log(N). The threshold is fixed for this iteration. Stop when no child is retained; raise if ten iterations exhaust without convergence. Original code has a hardcoded ten-iteration limit and inactive cap1000; no cap truncates this implementation. Full final binding converges, retaining217 high-pass and242 final candidates before mixing.

Sort retained objectives with NumPy argsort. For each of the best10 parents, every retained candidate, and q in[1e-7,1e-6,1e-5,1e-4,.001,.01,.1,1], append (wbest+q*wother)/(1+q). Branch number is the sum of parent branches. Preserve the original branch-zero scoring behavior: a branch-zero mixture repeats its own sigma clipping and updates the mask used by later branches. This detail is part of exact reference behavior, not an unadvertised posterior approximation. Append all mixtures, without an extra acceptance cut. Choose the minimum objective. Full counts are17577 high-pass and19602 final candidates.

After attenuation estimation, multiply original T by a, median-normalize and repeat the entire population search. All updates, clipping, initialization and stopping are local NumPy mathematics. Final output includes198 light fractions,2308 relative attenuations and one E. No absolute mass is inferred; template mass normalization cancels. Notebook maps weights to all age/metallicity nodes and shows near-optimal candidate degeneracy without claiming probability confidence intervals.
