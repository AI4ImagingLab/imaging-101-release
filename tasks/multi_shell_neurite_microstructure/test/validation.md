## Independent physics and selected numerical criteria

The orientation integral is independently evaluated by quadrature on the unit sphere, including equal-diffusivity and b0 limits; maximum disagreement with original response is3.33e-16. A known quadratic optimizer case has optimum(2,-1). The constant objective demonstrates equally optimal, non-unique coordinates. Fractions remain within[0,1], diffusivity within[0,0.00305]mm²/s and the two derived diffusivities satisfy the selected compartment relations at all supported voxels. Finite33-direction sampling is explicitly approximate, rather than claiming exact rotational invariance of this acquisition.

The original C++ initial/shrink stable_sort comparator uses <=, violating the generic strict-weak-order requirement. Independent inspection and captured cases identify the fixed libstdc++ small three-element insertion behavior: a newly inserted equal item precedes earlier equal items. The local bounded three-element insertion makes this observed convention explicit without copying undefined generic-sort behavior. Updated-worst insertion uses the source strict< rule. The stopping coordinate width uses original storage slots0 and2; it is not silently replaced by ranked best/worst or full-simplex diameter.

Numerical boundaries were frozen in prelocal_full_gate.json before local inverse extraction: every field NCC>=0.999999 and dynamic-range NRMSE<=1e-5. The independent source float32 nextafter output envelopes are approximately3.4e-8–6.1e-8. These nonzero numerical tolerances do not claim biological uncertainty, and were not enlarged to accommodate local outcomes. NCC is uncentered cosine similarity and NRMSE is RMS error divided by the supported reference max-minus-min. No scaling, registration, cropping or output-dependent masks are used.
