# Implemented dynamic virtual-fields inverse

The inverse unknown is pressure p in Pa on a thin steel plate. The public measurement w is optical transverse deflection in m, shape(1,250,79,97), float64. It is derived by the original author pipeline from all280 real camera images: the first30 form a low-noise unloaded reference; frames30:280 are observed. Original triangular grid demodulation, source phase unwrapping, mirror geometry, Gaussian slope smoothing sigma2raw pixels, downsampling5 and sparse slope integration with two bottom5x5corner displacement gauge produce this measurement. None uses pressure or the independently recorded impact force. These upstream-only acquisition preprocessing steps and their discrepancies are documented in validation; the shipped inverse begins from this measured w, not raw grid images.

Adopt source x=row direction and y=column direction, both with spacing Delta=0.0030065919701586503m. Source calibration evaluates Delta=5*1.02189781*(5.88433e-3/5)*0.5. Frame time is t_i=i/75000s, i=0..249, resetting origin to the first processed frame as in the source example. No time shift against the hammer is optimized. Positive pressure corresponds to the positive inertial acceleration of positive deflection. The material constants are E=190e9Pa, nu=0.3, rho=7934kg/m^3 and h=4.95e-3m. Flexural coefficients are D11=E h^3/[12(1-nu^2)] and D12=nu D11, in N m. These are original example constants; data-description rounding/distance discrepancies are explicitly retained, not silently corrected.

A temporal Gaussian with sigma6samples=80us filters w along time before any derivative. scipy.ndimage.gaussian_filter1d uses mode nearest and truncate4, exactly preserving the source. No spatial filtering or resampling is repeated in the inverse, because it has already occurred in the optically derived observation. Local slopes are s_x=-d_x w and s_y=-d_y w. Local curvatures are kxx=d_x s_x, kyy=d_y s_y and kxy=(d_y s_x+d_x s_y)/2. Temporal acceleration is a=d_t(d_t w). Each derivative uses centered interior differences and one-sided first-order edge differences via numpy.gradient; the factors Delta and dt=1/75000s appear in each differentiation. Thus slope is dimensionless(rad in small angle), curvature1/m and accelerationm/s^2. No spectral or continuum derivative replaces this finite scheme.

For an even window N=20, physical support length L=N Delta, let u_j=(j+1/2)/(N/2), j=0..N/2-1. Define h_left=3u^2-2u^3 and h_right=1-3u^2+2u^3. Concatenate the two halves to form h. Their first derivatives are plus/minus(6u-6u^2)/(L/2) and second derivatives plus/minus(6-12u)/(L/2)^2. This is the unit-center four-element Hermite16 patch with C1continuity and zero displacement/slope at the continuous perimeter. Sample virtual deflection v=outer(h,h), kxx_v=-outer(h'',h), kyy_v=-outer(h,h'') and kxy_v=-outer(h',h'). These explicit tensor products are algebraically equivalent to the original full16-node coordinate/reshape implementation and separately validated against its captured arrays. Midpoints are used, not the endpoints of the continuous patch.

For every time frame compute valid two-dimensional convolutions A11=conv(kxx,kxx_v)+conv(kyy,kyy_v)+2conv(kxy,kxy_v), A12=conv(kxx,kyy_v)+conv(kyy,kxx_v)-2conv(kxy,kxy_v) and M=rho h conv(a,v). The source uses convolution with180degree kernel reversal rather than correlation; the local code preserves convolve2d valid convention exactly. Pressure is p=(D11 A11+D12 A12+M)/sum(v). The common Delta^2sample area cancels from numerator and denominator, so no extra pixel-size factor enters p. The bending and inertial terms are also returned for scientific comparisons. Output has shape(1,250,79-20+1,97-20+1)=(1,250,60,78), float64 Pa. Patch center is at((r+9.5)Delta,(c+9.5)Delta) relative to measured-field origin. shift=False means no half-pixel interpolation. This is a closed-form weak-work load estimate: there is no optimizer, initialization, objective minimization, regularization iteration, step size, line search or stopping rule. Noise suppression is provided by the fixed temporal filter and virtual window, not hidden iterative optimization.

The continuum forward model is D11 Laplacian-squared(w)+rho h w_tt=p. Its local weak observation is sum(p*v)=D11 A11+D12 A12+M. physics_model.pressure_virtual_work explicitly implements the external-pressure side. The constant-within-window assumption gives the closed-form inverse above. No boundary padding appears in the spatial convolutions, and the output is defined only for valid patches; this declared reduction in array size is not an evaluator crop.
