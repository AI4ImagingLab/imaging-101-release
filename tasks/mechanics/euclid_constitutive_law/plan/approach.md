# Implemented mathematical approach

The acquisition is the entire author UT600 case:383 nodes,696 triangles,600 measured displacement fields,4 stored reaction records (two nonempty). Float64 CSV values are preserved. No target-derived feature fields or frozen normal matrices are public inputs. Author shape gradients and area weights are legitimate preexisting FE geometry calibration; nodal coordinates were rounded independently.

For triangle e, F=I+sum_a u_a outer gradNa, with row-major entries a,b,c,d. Set J=ad-bc, s=a^2+b^2+c^2+d^2, k=(d,-c,-b,a), I1=s+J^-2, I2=J^2+sJ^-2. Their derivatives with respect to the four F entries are dI1=2F-2k/J^3 and dI2=2Jk+2F/J^2-2sk/J^3. Compute descending in-plane singular values l1,l2 and l3=1/J. SVD singular-value derivatives are outer products of corresponding left and right singular vectors; dl3=-k/J^2. Equal singular-value contributions are combined symmetrically, so the isotropic summed feature derivative remains meaningful at equal stretches.

Q contains20 monomials K1^(p-q)K2^q (p1..5,q0..p), log(I2/3), then150 sums of stretches to alpha minus3, alpha=np.arange(-10,20,.2). Preserve nominal-zero floating exponent and absence of alpha^-2 normalization. The monomial product rule, dlog(I2)=dI2/I2, and alpha*l^(alpha-1)*dl give G=dQ/dF, shape(elements,171,4). W=Qtheta and P=sum_k theta_k G_k. This reduced derivative eliminates incompressibility pressure under zero P33, rather than dropping it from the in-plane constitutive response. W,P,theta all use MPa. Paper v1 Eq12's pressure term in stored energy conflicts with its Eq8 at J3D=1; W=Qtheta follows Eq8 and the author source.

Integrate nodal force operator A[n,i,k]=sum_e containing n area_e sum_j G[e,k,i,j] gradNa[e,n,j]. Force units are N/mm. Select free node/component rows and sum constrained rows into four reaction groups. Each frame contributes H=2(Afree.T Afree+20 R.T R), b=40 R.T r, C=20 r.T r. Accumulate all600 frames in author numeric order; do not store their giant feature tensors. Weak SSE is V=.5theta.T Htheta-b.T theta+C, with no frame or DOF normalization. The selected fixed-penalty objective is V+10000 sum(theta), theta>=0.

This is the paper's fixed-lambda sparse stage, not full Pareto selection or final unregularized model refinement. Native code's permanent coefficient pruning and600-fold penalty ranking fail qualification and are comparison-only. Their original and higher-iteration evidence remains private. Corrected reference retains the paper's fixed-point weighted-least-squares family while correcting positivity, reentry, convergence checking, and objective ranking; it is not unchanged native reproduction.

Condition the normal system with s_j=sqrt(Hjj), T=sqrt(C), u_j=s_j theta_j/T. Set Mij=Hij/(s_i s_j), v_j=b_j/(s_j T), r_j=lambda/(s_j T). Original objective divided by C is .5u.T M u-v.T u+1+r.T u. Initialize u from independent RandomState(seed).uniform(.01,.1); standard seed0. For floors eps=1e-3,1e-5,1e-7,1e-9,1e-11,1e-13 solve repeated nonnegative quadratic subproblems with matrix M+diag(r/max(u,eps)) and linear term v. This is a floored quadratic majorization of L1; the floor is progressively reduced and the final original objective is independently checked. Stop a floor stage when max coefficient change<1e-12, with cap3000 updates. Failure raises rather than silently accepting a partial iterate.

Each quadratic is solved by explicit primal-feasible working-set updates: start at zero, enter the inactive index with most negative gradient, solve the unconstrained passive principal system, and take the largest feasible segment toward it if any passive coefficient becomes negative; remove boundary-hit variables and repeat. Inactive variables can reenter at every subproblem. Quadratic gradient tolerance1e-12, active-set cap5000, numerical zero removal1e-15. No generic domain optimizer is used. Finally require max(abs(min(u,M u+r-v)))<=1e-8, the full projected KKT residual including every inactive variable. This numerical test uses dimensionless scaled variables and does not assert uniqueness of171 coefficients.
