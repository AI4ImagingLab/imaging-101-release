`humlicek` evaluates the author's four-region rational approximation W=H+iFfull to the complex Voigt function. Its branches use |x|+a at15 and5.5 and a>=0.195*|x|-0.176. Ffull is twice the conventional Faraday–Voigt profile. `profile` uses the original analytic identities dH/dx=2a*Ffull-2xH, dFfull/dx=2*(1/sqrt(pi)-aH-x*Ffull), dH/da=-dFfull/dx, dFfull/da=dH/dx. Since approximate Voigt values are used with analytic identities, finite differences of the rational approximation agree only to its approximation accuracy. Tests distinguish this from exact upstream response parity.

For each line, let Hpi,Hblue,Hred and their dispersion counterparts be its weighted profiles. Let t=theta, f=phi and E=gf*eta/2. The absorption coefficients are etaI=1+sum E*[Hpi*sin(t)^2+(Hblue+Hred)*(1+cos(t)^2)/2], etaQ=sum E*[Hpi-(Hblue+Hred)/2]*sin(t)^2*cos(2f), etaU with sin(2f), etaV=sum E*(Hred-Hblue)*cos(t). The rho coefficients use identical Q/U/V expressions with dispersion profiles. Both spectral lines contribute across both complete fine regions; no window truncation replaces their wings.

Write k=(etaQ,etaU,etaV), r=(rhoQ,rhoU,rhoV), h=etaI. Let g3=h^2+|r|^2, g1=g3-|k|^2, g2=k dot r, and D=h^2*g1-g2^2. The emergent intensity is S0+mu*S1*h*g3/D. The polarized vector is -mu*S1*[h^2*k-h*(k cross r)+r*g2]/D. This is the first-column analytic inverse of the public K matrix. `Dual` propagates the nine partial derivatives through these explicit operations and the analytic Voigt identities. This is local executable differentiation, with no domain solver or opaque transfer library.

## Objective, updates and termination

The minimized chi-square is sum over all Stokes/fine bins of [(observed-synthetic)/(sigma*sqrt(120))]^2. Each pixel has its own objective and damping. No spatial regularizer is included in this fit. The dimensionless variables are c=p/s, with s=(1000,1,1,1,0.05,10,0.1,1,1). Initial p=(1500,2.2,1,-0.5,0.035,50,0.1,0.24,0.7).

At the current best c, form residual r=(observed-synthetic)/(sigma*sqrt(120)) and response J=ds/dc/(sigma*sqrt(120)). Here the observation index is the column of J. Set A=J*J^T and b=J*r. Multiply each A diagonal by1+lambda and solve A*delta=b by column-pivoted Householder QR. The actual upstream Eigen solve uses numerical nonzero pivots at machine precision, despite a diagnostic `setThreshold(1e-9)` call: that threshold affects rank(), not solve(). The local QR uses its corresponding machine-precision nonzero-pivot test and sets dropped permuted variables to zero. A minimum-norm truncated GELSY solve would change these updates and is not used.

Propose the full step c+delta, convert to physical units, wrap azimuth once using the source float32 constants3.1415926 and3.1416026, and clip to lower(0,0,0,-13,0.001,0.01,1e-5,0,-10) and upper(6000,pi,pi,13,0.2,120,10,10,10). Accept only a strict decrease in chi-square. Initial lambda=sqrt(10). An accepted step divides lambda by sqrt(10), bounded below1e-4 and above100*sqrt(10); if already at its minimum the next value is0.001. Rejection multiplies lambda by10 and restores the residual at the unchanged best model. Stop after25 accepted updates, six consecutive rejections, best chi-square below1, or the second accepted fractional improvement below0.003.
