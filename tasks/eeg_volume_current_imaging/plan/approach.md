# Validated distributed current-field mathematics

The bound observation is the first1600 samples of real PhysioNet S001R04:64 electrodes at160 Hz, converted from original EDF calibration to float64 volts. There is no filtering, event selection, averaging or decimation. The independently defined source volume has263 Cartesian nodes with15 mm pitch inside a65 mm radius sphere centered(0,0,40) mm, with10 mm center exclusion and5 mm boundary clearance. A template standard1005 montage supplies electrode positions. Four-shell radii are(0.90,0.92,0.97,1.0)×90 mm and conductivities(0.33,1.0,0.004,0.33) S/m. The resulting G has shape(64,789), units V/(A m). Identity MRI/head transformation here is an explicit approximation, not subject-specific anatomy. No source answer is used to build G.

Let y be the64×1600 observation matrix, j the789×1600 vector of spatial dipole moments, and P=I−11ᵀ/64 the average-reference projector. Use sigma=10^-5 V as an ad-hoc isotropic covariance model; it is not a measured noise standard deviation. Define A=PG/sigma, b=Py/sigma, rank63, and q=||A||_F²/63. Original MNE uses isotropic source covariance1/q, loose=1, depth=None, fixed=False, rank={'eeg':63}, method='MNE', pick_ori='vector', nave=1, lambda2=1/9. No depth weights, cortical orientation prior or dSPM statistic is applied.

The objective is ||Aj−b||_F²+lambda2*q*||j||_F², with unnormalized sums and no extra1/M or1/2 factors. Compute reduced SVD A=U diag(s)Vᵀ and evaluate j=V diag[s/(s²+lambda2*q)]Uᵀb. The kernel is K=V diag[s/(s²+lambda2*q)]UᵀP/sigma. Both the full current field and the independently assembled MNE kernel are tested. This direct quadratic minimizer needs no initialization, line search or iterative stopping rule. Restore node-major Cartesian order(263,3,1600), prepend batch, and retain float64 A m units.

The local forward is PGj and its adjoint is GᵀPv. Analytic cases check adjointness, SI scaling, projection idempotence, reference nullspace and ridge stationarity. Independent full-current parity uses rtol2e-10 and atol1e-20 A m. Kernel comparison uses matrix relative norm1e-10: a preliminary pointwise test exposed tiny cancelling entries with absolute differences below4e-19 A m/V; the scale-aware norm explains that conditioning without changing the solver. The independently captured whitener and source covariance are also checked against P/sigma and1/q.

The Judge measures relative Euclidean error on every Cartesian component, every node and every time sample, boundary1e-7. It never fits a scale, mask or alignment. No independent spatial source truth exists. Forward residual is ||PGj−Py||/||Py||, and explained variance is1−residual², approximately0.799 for the original standard method. Zero fields fail the strict reference test and have no explanatory current distribution.

Review experiments actively recompute lambda2=1/900,1/90,1/9,1,10 on the same measured data and fixed geometry. They compare full current norm, electrode residual, spatial power and vector directions. This evaluates a reproducible approximate biophysical source inverse; it does not claim a precise cortical activation location or classify motor imagery.
