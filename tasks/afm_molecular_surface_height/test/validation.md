## Fixed scope and duplicate audit

Physical unknown: 30 nonnegative molecular sample height fields. Acquisition: finite-tip geometric contact maximum plus independent Gaussian height noise. Selected method: 1997 morphological surface erosion with an explicitly added known-zero-substrate projection. Main output: `reconstruction` real float64 shape (1,30,30,30) in Angstrom, without alignment. This differs from existing optical PSF deconvolution (linear convolution), crystal Bragg phase/strain retrieval (Fourier intensity), electron tomography (line integrals) and gradient-integration surface tasks: the max-plus contact operator and inaccessible-valley ambiguity are different. Same dataset/method swaps were excluded. Parent reserved `afm_molecular_surface_height` before task directory creation and authorized known independently specified probe input.

## Mathematical discrepancy and deliberate constrained variant

The modern original generic erosion uses same-offset unfold windows as its dilation. The general mathematical order adjoint requires reversed offsets for this forward convention. The local main operator therefore evaluates the explicit reversed-offset formula in the paper's reflected-cap convention. The known bound probe's active section is symmetric about(6,6), and its inactive skirt below−223Angstrom cannot contact the bounded data. Consequently the independently measured local/original main erosion and forward differences are0 at every bound pixel. This is documented as a convention limitation of the modern comparison, not a claim that two generic formulas are identical. Tests with an asymmetric3x3probe establish the local adjunction and an independently known impulse-contact example. The fresh blind comparison intentionally preserves the original same-offset convention to compare the actual published-modern implementation honestly.

The shipped `max(erosion,0)` adds the independently known zero substrate. It is explicitly labeled a constrained variant throughout README/plans/notebook; raw original erosion remains a separate fresh curve/map. It changes actual-truth NCC to0.9514423828199022 and NRMSE to0.03055631860849182. Re-dilation observation RMSE is3.018926125977721Angstrom, near the3Angstrom source noise. Raw erosion re-dilation has lowerRMSE2.708607...but a positive mean residual about1.4922Angstrom and negative substrate estimates. Projection is justified by physical substrate knowledge, not selected by hiding a failed standard run.
