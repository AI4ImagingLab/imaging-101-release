# Implemented mathematics

The unknown is the full effective relative HbO/HbR concentration c[b,p,j,t] in M (mol/L). The input has one batch, 68 paths, wavelengths 760 and 850 nm, and all 4860 samples at 8.928571428571429 Hz. Native float64 intensities and geometric calibration are preserved. Positive measured intensities need no repair; negative/zero inputs are rejected. No truth or target-derived calibration is used.

For each pair p and wavelength w, Ibar[p,w] is the arithmetic mean over the complete time axis. Optical density z[p,w,t]=-ln(I[p,w,t]/Ibar[p,w]) uses the natural logarithm. This convention is preserved from MNE; mean(z) generally is not zero. The spectral E[w,j] is the upstream Prahl coefficient table interpolated at nominal wavelengths and multiplied by 0.2303. E has units m^-1 mM^-1: the underlying decadic cm^-1 M^-1 table receives ln(10)/10 approximated by the original 0.2303 constant, which must not be silently recomputed. Distances d[p] are measured source–detector separation in m and f[w]=6.0 is the assumed dimensionless pathlength factor. A[p,w,j]=1000 E[w,j] d[p] f[w] maps M to dimensionless z. The upstream code first solves using E*d*f for mM and multiplies by 1e-3; the shipped algebra is equivalent.

The objective is L(c)=1/2 sum over w,t (A c-z)^2, independently for each path. Its gradient is A^T(Ac-z); no averaging factor, regularizer, positivity constraint, temporal filter, short-channel correction or spatial prior is used. Real concentration changes may have either sign. Both wavelengths are retained and A is nonsingular. For A=[[a,b],[g,h]], determinant D=ah-bg, the explicit minimizer is c0=(h z0-b z1)/D and c1=(-g z0+a z1)/D. The implementation directly evaluates this closed form in float64. There is no initialization, iterative update, step size, line search or stopping tolerance. Singular matrices and nonpositive pathlength factors raise errors. An adjoint implements the exact Euclidean transpose for independent tests.

Main execution always uses f=(6,6), and outputs (1,68,2,4860), HbO then HbR in M. Notebook comparisons actively compute altered pathlength factors, single-wavelength minimum-norm estimates, regularized two-wavelength estimates, temporal acquisition reduction and short-separation regression. These are sensitivity/limitation experiments, not the canonical method or alternate benchmark reference. In particular, the two-wavelength residual cannot validate biological correctness because the square system fits optical density exactly.

Evaluation uses independent upstream MNE results as method reference, not physiological truth. Every species separately receives cosine NCC without centering and RMS error normalized by its reference dynamic range. The forward consistency metric is ||A c-z||F/||z||F. No alignment, crop, calibration adjustment or output scale fitting occurs. Thresholds were fixed before local extraction: NCC>=0.999999999, NRMSE<=1e-9 and relative forward residual<=1e-10. These are nonzero float64 numerical tolerances appropriate to a closed-form reference task, not relaxed scientific quality boundaries. Full stage parity is tested at rtol=1e-12, atol=1e-16; independent unit/sign, adjoint, analytic inverse and baseline-gauge checks supplement parity.
