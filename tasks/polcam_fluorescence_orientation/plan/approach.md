# Implemented analyzer inverse

The unknown is the effective detector-coordinate linear Stokes vector S=(S0,S1,S2) in ADU at each acquired plane and 2x2 sensor tile. This is the selected no-interpolation option of the published napari-polcam diffraction-limited path, not a 3D dipole-spread-function fit. Raw I has shape (1,116,298,298), uint16. Float64 arithmetic begins before any differences or sums. Tile channels (I0,I45,I90,I135) are respectively raw positions (even,odd),(odd,odd),(odd,even),(even,even), using zero-based indices. A 135-degree linear analyzer is equivalent to -45 degrees. The coordinate origin is the deposited crop, columns right and rows down. No rotation or reindexing is introduced. The effective tile center is (2c+0.5,2r+0.5) raw pixels; nominal lateral pitch is 138 nm. Acquired planes retain their order; their physical spacing is unresolved.

Forward intensities are I0=(S0+S1)/2, I45=(S0+S2)/2, I90=(S0-S1)/2 and I135=(S0-S2)/2. The four-by-three real analyzer matrix A is given in README. For each tile the implemented objective is E(S)=||A S-I||_2^2/2. No spatial/axial regularizer is used. The factor one half is the stated objective normalization; changing it does not change the minimizer. A^T A=diag(1,1/2,1/2), so explicit least squares gives S0=(I0+I45+I90+I135)/2, S1=I0-I90 and S2=I45-I135. The gradient is A^T(AS-I). The local forward and adjoint implement these matrices exactly; tests check both the normal-equation residual and independent adjoint inner products. There is no initialization, step size, iteration, line search or stopping tolerance because this closed-form solution evaluates its formula directly.

The derived angle is phi=atan2(S2,S1)/2, in radians and principal interval [-pi/2,pi/2]. At exactly isotropic tiles atan2(0,0)=0 is the declared numerical convention. The derived degree is p=hypot(S1,S2)/abs(S0), dimensionless. With zero total and polarized signal p=0; a nonzero polarized signal at zero total is rejected. All bound observations yield positive S0. There is no clipping, intensity normalization or target-based correction. Nominal offset=0 ADU matches the original default. It describes an effective detector field, not an absolute photon/polarization calibration. Uniform added pedestal b shifts S0 by 2b while S1/S2 and phi remain unchanged. This identity is independently tested and explored on actual measurements as a sensitivity experiment. A physical black-level/gain map cannot be registered because source TIFF dimensions disagree with metadata ROI. No guessed calibration is used.

Reference arrays are the independently captured unmodified upstream values on all bound observations. They are method results, not actual biological truth. Stokes components and p each use cosine NCC and dynamic-range-normalized RMSE. Phi uses pi-periodic angular RMSE weighted by upstream S1^2+S2^2. The weight is fixed before submission and expresses orientation identifiability; no target-dependent spatial mask is selected. Forward observation consistency is ||AS-I||_F/||I||_F on all channels and tiles. The nonzero residual reflects noise and different sampled subpixel positions; it is not forced to zero. These deterministic formula comparisons use NCC>=0.9999999999, NRMSE<=1e-10 and angular RMSE<=1e-10 rad; observation residual must not exceed 1.1 times the upstream value, approximately 0.0184847067. The source stage tolerances were frozen before implementation. This tight numerical boundary validates the specified effective estimator, not molecular accuracy or a universal reconstruction technique.

Notebook comparisons actively compute fresh local output, load the independently captured upstream result, show raw channels, all Stokes components, orientation, degree and paired-channel residuals, display axial/orthogonal detector sections, and explore uniform detector-pedestal and spatial-averaging sensitivity. These are actual newly computed sensitivity results, distinct from the canonical offset-zero result. They do not replace the standard inverse or change evaluation. No 3D tilt, wobble, DSF fitting, super-resolution localizations or live-cell time-lapse are inferred from this single stack; those other paper experiments are explicitly outside its acquisition scope.
