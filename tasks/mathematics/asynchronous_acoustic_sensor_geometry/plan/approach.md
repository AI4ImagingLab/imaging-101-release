# Implemented mathematics

The selected method is the author's joint hybrid-TDOA Gauss–Newton calibration, run on the first real ten-channel/fourteen-event configuration. It estimates geometry and clocks together. There are ten microphone locations and fourteen source locations in metres, nine relative clock offsets in seconds, and ten dimensionless clock drifts. Source-clock columns in the 24-by-5 working array are unused padding, not additional unknowns.

The complete forward equations and physical clock approximation are stated in README. Internally the state columns are x, y, z, offset, drift. Six source-coordinate gauges and the first sensor offset are fixed. This leaves 85 free coordinates. The first source is zero, second source y/z are zero, and third source z is zero. No gauge alignment is performed after reconstruction. The positive orientation follows the supplied displacement frame; invalid reflected output is rejected by the Judge.

Residuals are prediction minus observation: 130 successive-event values ordered sensor then interval, 126 inter-sensor values ordered event then non-reference sensor, and 39 displacement values ordered event then xyz. The objective is half the variance-weighted sum of squared residuals. Weight covariance is diagonal: 256 entries of 1e-8 s² and 39 entries of 1e-4 m². These are the author's nominal 100 µs and 1 cm model weights, not inferred instrument noise. There is no regularizer, hidden geometric shape prior or normalization by sample count.

For distance between microphone m and source s, its microphone derivative is (m-s)/distance and the source derivative is its negative. The timing rows divide spatial derivatives by 340 m/s; successive drift derivatives are the known interval, inter-sensor offset derivatives are one, and the two drift derivatives are plus/minus cumulative emission time. Displacement derivatives are minus/plus the 3-by-3 identity. All 295 residuals and all 85 Jacobian columns are explicitly implemented in physics_model.residual_jacobian.

The undamped update is the negative weighted normal-equation step. To preserve actual source floating-point conventions, normal_step first solves the negative 85-by-85 normal matrix against the complete transposed Jacobian, then divides columns by the variances and multiplies by the residual. This matches the original MATLAB left-to-right backslash/right-division expression; solving directly against the final gradient has measurably different roundoff near ill-conditioned candidates.

The state is updated before testing the native stopping rule: mixed-unit free-step Euclidean norm below 0.01, or the pre-update objective below 30. A candidate diverges when pre-update objective grows above 1e5 times its initial value or becomes nonfinite. The dimensional inconsistency of the original mixed-unit norm is retained and disclosed; it is not a calibrated error bound. There is no line search. The original wall-clock cap is hardware dependent; the local implementation uses a portable maximum of 100 iterations. All selected upstream and local candidates terminate by the native stopping conditions, not this cap.

Thirty independently uniform, target-blind spatial initializations use xyz bounds [-0.8,0.8], [0,2], [-1,0] m, then impose the source-coordinate gauge. All clocks start at zero. NumPy default_rng seed20261029 defines a reproducible portable start library, supplied identically to the independent original oracle before local extraction. The original retry strategy is extended to a fixed 30 starts and chooses the smallest observed-data post-update objective among converged finite candidates. It never invokes the author's real_main GT-based quality selection. An independent seed20261030 library is a robustness comparison. Failed candidates are recorded rather than plotted as zero-cost results.

The main runtime fits clocks. Notebook fit_clocks=False is an explicitly labeled wrong-clock ablation that fixes all offsets/drifts to zero, retains the same geometry and observed inputs, and is not called the published TDOA-M comparator. The archived original TDOA-M comparator uses a surveyed microphone frame through sg_generation; it is labeled privileged and only compared by rigid-motion-invariant pairwise distances. It is not used to initialize the public reconstruction or to establish admissible solver superiority.

The output is only physical xyz. Judge timing consistency profiles the 19 linear clock nuisances using submitted geometry and the observations, without surveyed coordinates. Geometry NCC is uncentered cosine and NRMSE is RMS coordinate error divided by the coordinate range, separately for sensors and sources. Surveyed xyz are actual truth; upstream zero clock placeholders are not truth. Physical quality is decimetre-scale in this bound noisy configuration, not centimetre accuracy. Thresholds are fixed from two independent original runs using 0.9 times the smaller NCC, 1.1 times the larger NRMSE and 1.1 times the larger observed-consistency RMS. No rescale, Procrustes, magnitude conversion or crop is used.
