## Ambiguities, conventions and independent scientific checks

The source's published free-scale update v=(XPX-s*tr(A R))/(D mass) is valid at optimal scale. Reusing it with scale disabled omits the general quadratic term and is outside the selected task; no invalid rigid-only branch is silently copied. Independent known-transform tests establish signs, determinant and spatial scale. Row-vector R is the transpose of the paper's column-vector convention. The objective trace is explicitly the source EM surrogate evaluated with previous variance, not a mislabeled monotonic observed likelihood. Common normalization is undone analytically for every native source sample. Partial overlap and scanner sampling mean nearest-point distance is not exact pose truth.

No paper-reported metrics are relabeled as reproduced. This benchmark isolates the actual selected CPD similarity algorithm and real two-scan acquisition; it does not claim a complete reproduction of the paper's other datasets, affine/nonrigid algorithms or full comparator study. The source's synthetic translated-bunny demo was rejected because it makes the correspondence problem artificially trivial.
