For every complete3x3window w of9pixels, use the affine model alpha(i) approximately a(w)^T I(i)+b(w), with a a3vector and b a scalar. Minimize sum over windows of sum over i in w of squared affine residuals plus epsilon times||a||^2. Here epsilon=1e-7. Eliminate a,b exactly by linear least squares. With mu(w) the window mean and Sigma(w)=sum_i (I(i)-mu)(I(i)-mu)^T/9, the window contribution to L(i,j) is delta(i,j) minus[1+(I(i)-mu)^T (Sigma+epsilon I3/9)^(-1)(I(j)-mu)]/9 for pixels in that window. Delta is the Kronecker indicator and I3 is the3x3identity. Sum contributions from overlapping complete windows; do not pad, wrap, extrapolate or resize. The total objective is alpha^T L alpha, with no per-pixel/window averaging beyond the stated factors. The source comment calling L positive definite is imprecise: the constant vector is a null vector, so L is positive semidefinite; independent Schur-elimination/eigenvalue tests verify this without changing its formula.
