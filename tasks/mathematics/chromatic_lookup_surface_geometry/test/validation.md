## Numerical and physical discrepancies investigated

The primary depth is absolute axial mm, not camera range, disparity or a zero-offset height. No fitted scale, crop, alignment, blur or output mask is allowed. The low-level source3D-ray API uses index0 outside support whereas the4D image kernel uses−1; initial parity failure exposed this distinction and the local full-image code was corrected to the actual4D convention, without changing source depths or thresholds. Initial pytest fixture-path permission failures and that failed parity run remain archived. The final private short basetemp avoids Windows path/default-TEMP issues.

The four extra contexts show real limitations. Frames637/849 each have one supported nearzero depth(~1.08e−5/~2.56e−5mm) selected at calibration sample510. No geometric truth or result-filtered support is invented. Independent direct factor evaluation at those rays and neighboring control rays shows the other748depth samples are almost affine in stage index; their predicted sample510depth is~747mm. Float64 products give non510maximum residual<0.0001mm; actual float32 block products give<0.0002mm. The anomaly is in supplied calibration values, but its acquisition/compression/missing-support cause is unknown. calibration_trajectory_diagnostic.json/.npz and calibration_support_screen.json preserve the proof; neither original nor local calibration/output is corrected.

Separately, four extra-frame pixels show **numerical near-tie divergence**, not hidden primary parity success: frame425maximum depth difference0.22802734375mm and frame849maximum0.68389892578125mm. Private supplementary_near_tie_diagnostic.json/.npz repeats complete original-shape factor products and reproduces every original argmin, then independently reconstructs the actual local bounded block and reproduces each alternative argmin. RGB product differences2.38e−7..4.17e−7 flip squared-objective gaps0..8.85e−9 at adjacent few samples. The original and fresh fields and those discrepancies are retained in the active notebook. They are **not claimed to meet the bound three-frame0.001mm criterion**, and no limits were changed. The three originally preselected bound observations pass all frozen full-field criteria. This explicitly limits reproducibility beyond the bound acquisition/backend rather than silently claiming universal bitwise extraction.

The camera XYZ helper is supported by independent analytic geometry, not independent measured object truth. Photometric consistency can remain good at an aliased depth; near-ties, generated perturbations, rank changes and extra calibration failures show why reference agreement alone is not physical accuracy.
