## Reference roles and limitations

There is no actual sharp ground truth. evaluation/baseline_reference.npz stores the independently executed standard method. notebooks/reference_outputs/upstream.npz is consumed by notebook comparison and kernel inspection; its method result is explicitly labeled. test/fixtures/primitives.npz and stages.npz are independent native/captured original cases consumed by test/comparing_test/test_upstream.py. No fixture is generated by cleaned code. Full original .mat duplicates remain in the private archive only.

The author-supplied precomputed blind-deconvolved green image is not observation, truth, or primaryinput. The selected use_blind0 branch intentionally uses the measured green. Iteration and parameter comparisons are freshly reconstructed. Color images are clipped solely for display; full signed arrays enter metrics and Judge. Numerical agreement is not optical accuracy; no true-scenePSNR is invented.
