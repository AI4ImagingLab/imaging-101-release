# Implemented mathematics

The selected method is the original CBORT Tomogram.heterodyne2channel Fourier reconstruction of the complete measured human coronary B scan. It is a direct inverse; no learned weights, optimization, invented training, or stopping rule is involved. The two outputs are complex bandlimited backscatter images rather than histological truth.

Let c=0,1 denote detector channel; n=0,...,1599 native sweep sample; a=0,...,1023 lateral A-line; f denote a discrete Fourier bin. Input I has shape(2,1600,1024), ADU. Independent background b has shape(2,1600), ADU. First set g to int64 I, then assign g[:]=g-b[:,:,None]. NumPy truncates fractional ADU toward zero on assignment. This is original integer-ADC behavior, not an intentional denoising improvement. Alternative float subtraction is a documented sensitivity experiment, not the nominal algorithm.

The DFT is F(g)[f]=sum_n g[n] exp(-2pi i f n/1600), with no normalization. Retain f=0,...,799 in complex64 storage, append zeros to length3200, and roll by−400. Compute v=3200 IFFT(padded) with NumPy's normalized IFFT. This selects the positive-frequency heterodyne term and restores the original unnormalized analytic-fringe amplitude convention. No factor2 is inserted.

The public chirp vector t_j is the original normalized detector mapping: t=(mapping+1-weight)/max(mapping+1-weight). At grid l/800, interpolate t linearly from grid j/800. Force interpolated endpoints0and1. Multiply by3200, floor, force endpoints1and3199, and use searchsorted on integer grid1,...,3200. The resulting indices start0and end3198. Preserve this off-by-one convention as verified against original executable code. Calibration was not estimated from tissue reconstruction.

For dispersion, combine the first800 float32 values and second800 values into real+imaginary complex correction D_j, and interpolate linearly on the same fractional grids. Let s_c(l,a)=v_c(index_l,a)D_l. The Hann window is w_l=0.5[1-cos(2pi l/799)], l=0,...,799. The digital depth profile before display is h=10^-6 FFT_2048(w s), using zero extension of800 spectral samples. Roll depth by+1024 and reverse the complete depth axis. Save complex64(1,2,2048,1024). There is no log compression, magnitude conversion, crop, spatial interpolation, or normalization in the saved scientific result. Units are the original digital amplitude scale; an absolute reflectivity coefficient is not identifiable from the deposited optical calibration.

The forward model in the README is single-scattering heterodyne interferometry; the numerical inverse maps finite-bandwidth observations to a Hann-apodized depth representation. The reverse numerical operator unflips depth, unrolls−1024, divides by10^-6, and applies normalized inverse FFT. Its first800 samples reproduce w s and remaining1248 samples are zero within float32 FFT error. This independently checks sign, scale, support, and orientation. A synthetic analytic single-tone unit test fixes the expected depth bin and Parseval energy without generating benchmark observations.

Parity tolerances were frozen before local extraction: stages rtol2e-6/atol0.1ADU-derived spectral units; complete complex image rtol2e-5/atol2e-5 digital units. Nominal local output is exactly equal to the original. Judge relative-complex-L2 boundary2e-5 is a numerical precision budget, independent of anatomy accuracy. Two original reruns are deterministic; onefloat32ULP perturbation and complex64 roundtrip are reported in validation. Float32 FFT roundtrip is judged normwise at5e-7 because Hann endpoints have exactzero signal and pointwise relative error there is undefined. Measured nominal norm error is8.6605e-8.

Review comparisons actively reconstruct no-dispersion and rectangular-aperture alternatives on the same observations. These are sensitivity ablations, not unexecuted competing methods or claims of superior biological truth. Computed B scans, spectral power, depth power, phase-channel maps, image differences, and profile metrics expose the effects of calibration/apodization. The supplied upstream repository has no comparative notebook for the chosen example; its other tissue and polarization-derived contrasts are different datasets/inverse quantities and are explicitly outside this selected tomography task.
