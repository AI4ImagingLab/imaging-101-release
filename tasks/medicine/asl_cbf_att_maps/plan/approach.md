# Implemented kinetic inverse

The physical unknowns are voxelwise blood flow f in mL/g/ms and arterial transit time a in ms. M0 is independently supplied equilibrium magnetization. Seven observations y use labelling durations tau=(100,100,150,150,400,800,1800) ms and post-labelling delays w=(170,270,370,520,670,1070,1870) ms, with total time t=tau+w. Only the first acquired echo, TE=13.56 ms, is used, matching the upstream CBF mapper's `pcasl[0]` selection. No echo correction is introduced.

Let alpha=.85 be labelling efficiency, T=1650 ms blood longitudinal relaxation, lambda=.98 mL/g water partition coefficient, and Te=1/(1/T+f/lambda) the effective time constant. The forward signal is the response of a rectangular delivered bolus:

```math
s=2\alpha M_0 f e^{-a/T}\int_a^{\min(t,a+\tau)}e^{-(t-v)/T_e}\,dv
```

when t>=a, and zero before arrival. The integration variable v is arrival time. During delivery this is 2 alpha M0 f Te exp(-a/T)(1-exp(-(t-a)/Te)); after delivery it is 2 alpha M0 f Te exp(-a/T)(1-exp(-tau/Te)) exp(-(t-tau-a)/Te). This formula includes perfusion-dependent washout f/lambda. All exponents are dimensionless. The independent physics test evaluates the integral by numerical quadrature, rather than comparing two copies of the closed form.

The grid is exactly (z,y,x)=(5,35,35), at the supplied header's 1 mm spacing. No spatial discretization of a differential operator is required because the selected kinetic model is local to each voxel. There is no voxel interpolation, convolution, smoothing, intensity clipping or spatial regularizer. The supplied binary mask limits fitting to 5800 voxels and fixes exterior outputs at zero. Input float32 arrays are copied without precision or scale conversion.

For every masked voxel minimize the unweighted objective sum over the seven timing pairs of (s(f,a)-y)^2. No 1/N or 1/2 factor is inserted; SciPy's least-squares implementation internally uses half the sum of squares, with the same minimizer. The data variance is unknown, so this is unweighted residual minimization, not a calibrated chi-square likelihood. Initialize (f,a)=(1e-5,1000). Bounds are (0,0) to (1,5000). These are the upstream mapper defaults, including its very broad flow upper bound; they are numerical method parameters, not acquisition information. No claim is made that every fitted voxel is physiologically plausible.

`scipy.optimize.curve_fit` with bounds selects trust-region reflective nonlinear least squares (`least_squares(method='trf')`). No Jacobian is supplied; its finite-difference Jacobian uses the default two-point scheme, tolerances ftol=xtol=gtol=1e-8, and default maximum function evaluations 100*n=200 for n=2 parameters. No custom step size or line search replaces the selected upstream optimizer. A `RuntimeError` produces zero flow and ATT at that voxel, preserving the original behavior. Other input errors propagate. The production fit does not change calibration parameters.

Each fitted raw parameter is explicitly rounded to float32 before returning maps, matching upstream shared-memory `Array('f')` storage. Returned arrays are float64: CBF=6000000*f in mL/100g/min, ATT=a in ms. The flow factor is 100 g per g times 60000 ms per minute. This uses the upstream raw `cbf` result, not its optional `cbf_norm`, whose source multiplier 60*60*1000 is inconsistent with the stated units. Fitting parity is preserved; this dimensional correction is explicitly isolated and tested. The model's actual default lambda=.98 is retained; the mapper does not pass the separate MRIParameters Lambda field into its point model.

The notebook actively computes the same standard output, two alternative labelling efficiencies (.75,.95), and an alternative initial ATT=500 ms to expose calibration and initialization sensitivity. These comparisons use fresh local fits and are not shipped as additional benchmark answers. The optional single-delay closed form assumes complete arrival and negligible perfusion washout and is clearly labeled an approximation; its formula is documented in its function docstring. It uses the last acquired delay and is not the standard runtime.

Evaluation compares both complete maps to the independently executed standard method. NCC is uncentered cosine similarity; NRMSE is RMS error divided by reference dynamic range. No masks, crops, normalization or alignment affect these two map comparisons; exterior zero values are included. Forward consistency uses all seven timings inside the supplied mask, as relative signal L2 error. Baseline-relative fidelity is not a claim of independently known perfusion truth. Repeated original runs are identical; nonzero map tolerance accommodates float32 storage and independently assessed rounding. The fixed residual boundary is 1.01 times the upstream baseline residual and cannot be adjusted by the solver.
