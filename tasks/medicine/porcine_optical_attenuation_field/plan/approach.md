## Acquisition and restoration

The continuous single-scattering signal is C f(z)h(z)mu(z,x)exp(−2integral_0^z mu(s,x)ds), with unknown depth-independent scale C, double-pass factor2, and additive observation contamination. Discrete measurements represent depth-bin integrals. h=1/[1+((z−z0)/zR)²]. Spectral f=(sin(zeta)/zeta)² exp[−w² zeta²/(2log2)], w=resolution/pixel_wavelength, zeta=pi z/(2ranging_depth). Wavelength1.325e-6m,pixel spacing7.1875e-11m,resolution1.1055e-10m; ranging_depth=wavelength²/(4pixel_spacing) convertedm→mm with1000. np.sinc(zeta/pi) correctly supplies the sin(zeta)/zeta limit1.

Let A=observed I×support; noise parameter P=2.5e-5(squared normalized instrument units). The source restoration signal J=A/(h f) × A²/(A²+P). The squared-intensity shrinkage suppresses weak signal before depth integration; simple division by optics alone would amplify additive contamination. P is the declared author parameter, not newly measured or assumed spatial noise variance. Scale covariance is explicit: multiplying I byk and P byk² scales J byk and leaves the reconstructed attenuation unchanged.
