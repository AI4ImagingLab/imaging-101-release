# Implemented analytic diffusion standard

The public problem defines all equations and units. Three-phase demodulation is explicit float32 arithmetic, with sqrt(2)/3 times the square root of pairwise squared differences. At f=0 use DC, at all four nonzero f use AC. Reflectance is independently supplied Rcal times hand/phantom ratio. Preprocessing keeps source float32 frame precision and original Fortran array order before means; Rcal remains stored float64 but its scalar multiplication follows the original single-precision image convention. Each500 by400 calibrated channel is reshaped50 by 10 by 40 by 10 and averaged across the inner column then row dimensions, as in upstream rebin. No invalid cell is removed and no interpolation is performed.

The physics is the homogeneous half-space diffusion response from Cuccia2009, implemented in `diffuse_reflectance`. Frequencies are cycles/mm, so 2*pi*f is inverse mm and coefficients are inverse mm. Transport=muA+muSPrime, effective attenuation=sqrt(3muA*transport+(2*pi*f)^2). Dividing by transport produces dimensionless h. Boundary factor A=(1-r)/(2(1+r)), r=-1.440/n^2+.710/n+.668+.0636n, n=1.4. The numerator is3A(muSPrime/transport). The denominator is(h+1)(h+3A). The source's legacy wavelength coordinate1 is used only to interpolate a constant initialization table; wavelength does not occur in this equation and1nm is not an acquisition claim.

`reconstruct(observed,frequencies,index=1.4,frequency_indices=(0,1))` processes each50 by40 cell independently with the exact selected classical `fitOps(model='diff')` convention. Minimize sum of the two selected squared reflectance residuals, not its mean or a weighted/noise-normalized version. Initial(muA,muSPrime)=(.02,1)permm, Nelder-Mead, maxiter300,xatol=.001,fatol=.001; remaining general SciPy settings are defaults. No positivity projection, smoothness or bounds are inserted into the source trajectory. Nonconvergence, nonfinite or nonpositive final fields raise an error. The actual bound dataset yields positive converged solutions for every cell. The returned reflectance is recomputed from the final fields.

No actual optical truth exists. The independent author-code run supplies a standard field. Acceptance compares every cell's selected-two-frequency squared residual to1.02 times the standard residual plus1e-10 absolute floor, frozen before cleaned comparison. This permits improved continuous fits and avoids enforcing simplex-path identity. The reported reflectance must also match the submitted fields at relative L2 tolerance1e-9. All values and metrics must be finite; actual truth, if later provided, takes precedence and needs explicitly configured truth tolerance.

The notebook actively runs the complete bound reconstruction. All five frequency residual maps and a transport-validity indicator sqrt(3muA*transport+(2*pi*.4)^2)/transport are shown. This indicator is diagnostic, not a binary validity guarantee. Two additional full local fits at assumed n1.35 and1.45 probe model-convention changes, with all data and other settings unchanged. They are not measured refractive-index errors and do not replace the n1.4 task. No sample-derived calibration or inverse lookup table is used.
