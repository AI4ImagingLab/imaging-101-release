## Model and variable projection

Let N=3, TE=(.00287,.00607,.00927)s, dt=mean(diff(TE)), gamma42.576MHz/T, B01.494T. Chemical shifts are float32 c=(4.64655,5.3,4.31,2.76,2.1,1.3,.9)ppm, with water shift formed by1.3+3.748−.01085*37 before float32 conversion. The two-row float32 spectral mixture matrix alpha has alpha[0,0]=1 and alpha[1,1:]=(.048,.039,.004,.128,.693,.087), other entries0. Do not renormalize the fat row. M=2 coefficient components are complex; no real-valued2020 method is used by the shipped reconstruction.

For decay R in s^-1 the complex64 design matrix is A[n,m]=sum_p alpha[m,p]*exp(i2pi*gamma*B0*(c[p]-c[0])*TE[n]−R*(TE[n]−TE[0])). Model coefficients are normalized at the first echo. A field label b=0,...,99 gives diagonal modulation B[b,n,n]=exp(i2pi*b*n/100). Its ppm spacing is d=1/(abs(dt)*100*B0*gamma); positive precession, no conjugation. The complete alias period is100*d ppm. Compute complex64 Moore–Penrose pseudoinverse Ap=pinv(A), projection P=I−A*Ap, field-specific null projector B*P*conj(B), and coefficient inverse Ap*conj(B). SciPy/domain MRI inverses are not used; NumPy general linear algebra provides pinv and matrix products for the explicit local model.

The author implementation scales flattened observations Y by s=norm(Y)^2 to avoid intermediate overflow, retaining its complex64 in-place arithmetic. The local caller gives the core an independent copy, so no supplied observations are mutated. Each complex residual is Re(Y.conj().T *P*Y), summed over echoes. Field residual J[b,v] minimizes that residual over the selected provisional R=40s^-1. Full final decay search tests145 values R=0,1,...,144s^-1. ## Field labeling and coarse-to-fine updates

At every voxel, find strict cyclic local minima of J along field label b using J[b]<J[b−1] and J[b]<J[b+1], with modulo100. Sort by voxel then residual to select the two best local minima A and B, duplicating the best when only one exists; retain the source zero label when none exists. This tie/minimum convention is preserved exactly.

The field energy is sum_v J[label_v,v] plus sum_(v,u) w[v,u]*V(abs(label_v−label_u)). V(k)=min(k,100−k)^2 is cyclic squared label distance. Nearest positive-axis neighbors in x,y,z are included; physical distances are the voxel spacings, with no wrap at edges. The curvature proxy ddJ[v]=J[A+1,v]+J[A−1,v]−2J[A,v] supplies weights w=mu*min(ddJ[v],ddJ[u])/distance(v,u), mu=.1. All boundary-edge weights are explicitly0. No additional off-resonance penalty is part of the selected method; that optional upstream API is outside the shipped runtime.

Multiscale recursion chooses factors1or2 along nonsingleton dimensions to maximize hypervolume/hypersurface divided by the hypercube side-length scale. Coarse shape is ceil(fine/factors), spacing multiplies by factors. Residuals are zero-padded on high ends for odd dimensions, reshaped into blocks and averaged. This is a numerical source convention, including its padded-block normalization; do not substitute trilinear interpolation or averaging only valid samples. A one-voxel coarsest level takes its minimum. Propagation repeats each coarse label by ceil(fine/coarse) and truncates high ends to the original shape.

For binary candidates A_v/B_v, unary terms are J[A_v,v] and J[B_v,v]. Each explicit pair table contains w times the four possible cyclic distances V(A_v−A_u),V(A_v−B_u),V(B_v−A_u),V(B_v−B_u) with table index first at voxel v=q and second at neighbor u=p. The unmodified source passes endpoints(p,q), erroneously exchanging the mixed states; the qualified implementation passes(q,p) to match the table. `QPBO` constructs this generic binary polynomial term by term, then calls thinqpbo0.1.5 solely to optimize that binary energy. Its output0/1 fixes the corresponding candidate; negative/unlabeled entries retain the propagated coarse label. No water–fat physics, spectrum, candidate generation, multiscale rules or inverse update is hidden in this dependency.

Then10 source conditional-update sweeps refine labels. Each sweep snapshots the previous labels and tests offsets in order0,−1,+1,−2,+2,...,−10,+10 modulo100. Candidate costs include unary and both incident-neighbor terms, keeping the first candidate on an exact tie (`cost<min_cost`). This is the actual source simultaneous/snapshot update, not a replacement sequential coordinate descent. The implementation preserves it even though an arbitrary parallel conditional sweep need not monotonically decrease the full global energy. No optimization/convergence claim beyond the specified finite10sweeps is made. This entire recursion runs on the complete101x101x4 volume; the example config's optional ROI is not used.

Given final labels, choose the minimum complex residual over145 decay candidates, then apply the corresponding linear inverse matrix to the complex observations and multiply coefficients by s. Return water=abs(rho[0])float32. Aggregate fat is abs(sum(alpha[1,1:])*rho[1]) with the author's complex128 aggregation, then float32 storage. The supplied fat weights sum to approximately.999 rather than1; omitting this factor changes fat and fraction and is prohibited. Fat fraction is100*fat/(water+fat+float64epsilon), evaluated before its final float32 storage. Fieldmap=b*d and r2star=selected_rate are float64. No crop, padding, clipping, fitted rescaling or hidden alignment follows.
