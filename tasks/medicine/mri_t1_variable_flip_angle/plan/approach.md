# Implemented mathematics and conventions

Unknowns are T1 in ms and equilibrium magnetization M0 in scanner arbitrary units on the full original 128×128 single-slice grid. Measured S[j] at nominal angles α=[3,20] degrees follows spoiled steady-state GRE S[j]=M0 sin(a[j])(1−E)/(1−E cos(a[j])), E=exp(−TR/T1), TR=15 ms, and actual a[j]=B1 α[j]π/180. Independent B1 calibration is supplied per voxel; it is not estimated from hidden T1. The observed magnitude model assumes effective spoiling and steady state. TE-dependent scaling is absorbed into M0, so M0 is not an absolutely calibrated proton density.

The implemented qmrpy T1VFA default fit uses ordinary least squares after linearization x[j]=S[j]/tan(a[j]), y[j]=S[j]/sin(a[j]), y=c+E x. Let xbar,ybar be arithmetic means over the two measurements. E=Σ(x−xbar)(y−ybar)/Σ(x−xbar)² and c=ybar−E xbar. The objective is Σ[y[j]−c−E x[j]]², with no averaging factor and no spatial regularizer. T1=−TR/log(E), M0=c/(1−E). The implementation preserves upstream clipping E to [10⁻¹²,1−10⁻¹²]. On the bound supported data all estimates already lie strictly inside (0,1), so clipping does not affect results. This is a closed-form inverse: no initialization, inverse iteration, optimizer, learning rate, line search or stopping rule applies.

The original supplied mask has 4668 voxels. Both maps are exactly zero outside it; no inferred target mask or new threshold is used. Original input values are preserved as decoded float64 with original x,y order, removing only the singleton slice axis. No image rescaling, rotation, denoising or interpolation is performed. NIfTI spatial pitches are 2,2,1.9999983310699463 mm. The model is voxelwise, so no spatial boundary condition or FFT convention is involved.

`reconstruct_uncorrected` actively applies the same inverse with B1=1 for a scientific comparison. This does not replace the corrected task result. The notebook compares both fresh corrected and uncorrected maps to independently reproduced upstream counterparts, and shows their difference. It does not substitute archived measurements for a fresh comparison.

Reference evaluation uses a standard-method result from independent qmrpy source execution, not physical truth. Complete maps are compared using uncentered cosine NCC and RMS/reference dynamic-range NRMSE, without alignment or fitted intensity scales. Both T1 and M0 must have NCC≥0.999999 and NRMSE≤10⁻⁶. Independently computed GRE signal residual RMS divided by the measured signal dynamic range on the supplied mask must also be at most 10⁻⁶. This additional check ties both submitted quantities to public observations; two measurements interpolated by two fitted parameters still do not prove model accuracy. Float64 stage parity was fixed at rtol=10⁻¹⁰ before implementation; differences from centered scalar least-squares and vectorized arithmetic are assessed explicitly. Independent analytic steady-state and inverse roundtrip checks establish the units, angle convention and equations. Reference agreement establishes extraction fidelity, not clinical accuracy or the adequacy of spoiling assumptions.
